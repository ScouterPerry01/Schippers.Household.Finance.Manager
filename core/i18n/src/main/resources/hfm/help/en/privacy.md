# Privacy

Your financial information stays on your computer and your phone, encrypted. RANN does not receive or collect it. There is no RANN account, no advertising and no analytics.

## What stays on this computer

The household is a folder of encrypted files on your computer. It opens only with a user's password or recovery key. Documents you import are kept encrypted in the vault and read on this computer. Backups stay encrypted too. Only Export all data… makes an unencrypted copy, and the app warns you first.

## When the app goes on the internet

Nothing is sent anywhere except in these cases, and most are off until you turn them on:

- Exchange rates from the Bank of Canada, when your household uses another currency.
- A second source of exchange rates, if you turn it on under Rates and prices.
- Price downloads you turn on under Rates and prices. Each one sends the symbols of what you hold, nothing else about you or your amounts.
- Watch-only wallet updates you ask for.
- AI reading, with your own Anthropic key. Only the pages you approve are sent, after you crop them and hide what you choose.
- The update check, if you agreed and your copy checks for updates itself. GitHub sees your internet address, but nothing about your household.

## Your phone

RANN's Roost Mobile talks only to this computer, over your home Wi-Fi, encrypted between the two. Away from home, it can leave its captures, encrypted, in a folder of your own cloud service; that service only sees files it cannot read. See the help topic on sending from the phone.
