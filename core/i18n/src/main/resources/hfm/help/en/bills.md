# Bills

Bills tracks everything that comes back on a schedule: rent or mortgage, utilities, insurance, subscriptions, pay and regular transfers. It is in the Money group of the menu.

The screen has five tabs: To pay, All bills, Calendar, Subscriptions and Cash flow forecast.

## Add a bill

Choose Add a bill. Pick the type: Bill, Income or Transfer. Enter the name, the payee, the amount and the account it is paid from (or deposited to). Say whether the amount is fixed, variable or estimated, then set how it repeats, the first due date and, if you like, a last due date. Under Remind me (days before), enter for example 7, 1. Tick Subscription to follow it on the Subscriptions tab.

## Pay or skip

To pay lists what is overdue, due today and due in the next 30 days.

- Mark paid, or Mark received for income, adds the transaction to the account.
- Enter amount sets this time's amount for a variable or estimated bill.
- Skip passes over one occurrence.
- Undo, under Paid recently, reverses a payment you marked by mistake.

A warning appears when the paying account would go below zero.

## Subscriptions and forecast

Subscriptions shows each subscription with its yearly cost and the total for all of them. If you set a Cancel by (reminder) date, it shows there too. Cash flow forecast projects each account's balance from your bills over the next 30, 60 or 90 days. It shows today's balance, the balance at the end and the lowest point, and warns of payments that would overdraw the account.

## Calendar export

Export calendar… saves your bills as a calendar file, so you can add the due dates to another calendar program. Bills also appear on the Calendar.
