# Registered plans

Registered plans keeps track of contribution room, withdrawals, RESP grants, pensions and beneficiaries for each person in the household. It is in the Investing and borrowing group of the menu. Choose the year at the top right.

Room, penalties and minimum withdrawals are worked out from the figures you enter. They are an organizational aid, not tax advice. Your notice of assessment and your plan's issuer have the final word.

## Contribution room

The Contribution room tab shows room per person for the RRSP, TFSA and FHSA.

- Click Enter the CRA's figure and copy the number from your notice of assessment or My Account for exact results.
- Click Contribution outside the books for a contribution made through payroll, or securities moved in kind.
- A warning appears if a plan goes over its room, with the 1% a month the CRA charges on the excess.

## RRIF and LIF

This tab shows the minimum withdrawal for each RRIF or LIF, based on the plan's value on January 1 and the age at the start of the year, and what was withdrawn. Click Plan details for more.

## RESP

The RESP tab shows contributions per beneficiary across all RESPs, the grants the rules give (CESG, and the QESI in Quebec or the BCTESG in British Columbia), and those received. Click Record a grant received when a grant arrives.

## Pensions

Click Add a pension for an employer pension plan, QPP or CPP, or Old Age Security. Use Statements to enter each year's figures from the pension statement, such as the pension adjustment.

## Beneficiaries

Record who receives each plan at death with Add. This is for reference only; the designation made with the institution is what counts.
