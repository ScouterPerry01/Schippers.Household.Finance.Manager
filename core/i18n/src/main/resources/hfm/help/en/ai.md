# AI reading

Some documents are hard to read on this computer, such as a blurry receipt or an unusual statement. AI reading lets Claude, Anthropic's AI, read them, using your own Anthropic account and key. It is off until you turn it on. This screen is in Settings, under AI reading.

Only the pages you approve are sent, after you crop them and hide what you choose. Anthropic receives them to read them, and nothing else about your household leaves this computer. Each reading costs a few cents on your Anthropic account.

## Turn it on

- Create a key at console.anthropic.com, under API keys.
- Paste it in API key and click Save key. The key stays on this computer, for your user only, in Windows Credential Manager or your desktop's keyring.
- Click Check key (free) to make sure it works.
- Tick Read hard documents with AI.
- Choose a Model. Each one shows its price per million tokens.

Leave Show me each document and let me hide parts before it is sent ticked if you want to review every page first. Hidden areas, such as a full account number, are replaced by flat blocks before the picture leaves the computer.

## Read a document

In Documents, open the document and click Read with AI. Check the fields it fills in before you save them.

## Follow the cost

Under Usage, pick a Period (This month, This year or Everything) to see each request, its tokens and its estimated cost in US dollars.

## Stop using it

Click Remove key to delete the key from this computer, or untick Read hard documents with AI.
