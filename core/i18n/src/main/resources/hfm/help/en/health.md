# Health

Health is an organizer for your family's health information: medications, appointments, conditions, allergies, tests, vaccines and the people who care for you. It is in the Home and family group of the menu. The app does not give medical advice.

## Choose the person

Pick someone from the Person or pet list at the top. Pets are in the list too. If the list is empty, first add the people in your household under Household members, in the Settings group.

## Medications and refills

- On the Medications tab, choose Add a medication. Enter the dose, how to take it, who prescribed it, the pharmacy and the prescription number.
- Under Refills, enter when it was last filled, the days of supply and the refills remaining. The app then shows the next refill date.
- Use Remind me (days before) to choose how early you are reminded before the supply runs out.
- When you pick up a refill, choose Record a refill. The app shows when a prescription needs renewal.

## Other records

Each tab has its own add button: Add an appointment, Add a condition, Add an allergy, Add a test and Add a vaccine. For a test you can note the result, the normal range and a follow-up date. For a vaccine you can note when the next dose is due.

## Providers

On the Providers tab, choose Add a provider for your doctors, dentist, pharmacy and clinics.

## Health summary

Choose Health summary… to save a printable summary for one person, with their allergies, current conditions, medications and vaccines. The PDF can be protected with a password. The summary is not available for pets.
