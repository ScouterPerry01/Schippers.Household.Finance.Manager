# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after opening the household. It shows where things stand today and what needs your attention. It sits at the top of the menu, before the groups.

## Getting started guide

In a new household, a Getting started guide appears at the top. It lists a few steps: the people in the household, your accounts, your bills and pay, a first statement, and the phone (optional). Each step has a button that opens the screen that does it. The guide goes away once the steps are done, or you can choose Hide this guide.

## The tiles

A row of tiles gives the main numbers. Click a tile to open the screen behind it.

- Net worth, with the change since last month. Opens Reports.
- Cash available and Owing on credit and loans. Open Accounts.
- Bills overdue or due in the next 7 days. Opens Bills.
- Spent this month, compared with your budgets, and how many categories are over budget. Opens Budgets.

## Needs your attention

This list gathers what is waiting for you: overdue bills, statement lines that need a decision, transactions with no category, accounts not reconciled in more than 45 days, no successful backup in the last 7 days, and missing exchange rates. Click a line to go to the screen where you can deal with it. When the list is empty, it says Nothing to review.

## Charts

At the bottom, a line chart shows your net worth month by month, and Top spending this month ranks your largest categories. Click the spending bars to open Reports for more detail.
