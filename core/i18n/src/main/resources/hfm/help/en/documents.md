# Documents

Documents keeps your receipts, bills, statements and other papers in the encrypted vault. They are read on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose AI reading. Documents is in the Money group of the menu.

The screen has three tabs: To review, All documents and Old documents.

## Add documents

- Drop files on the screen, or choose Import files…. PDF, JPEG, PNG and HEIC files are accepted.
- Choose Watched folder… to pick a folder where your scanner or downloaded e-bills land. Files saved there are imported automatically. Stop watching ends this.
- Receipts and bills sent from your phone also arrive in To review. A transfer file from the phone, saved from an email or copied by USB, can be dropped here too.

## Review the inbox

New documents wait in To review. Choose Review to open one. Check the details that were read, correct them if needed, then file the document:

- File it with a recorded transaction of the same amount, using Attach.
- Or choose New transaction from this document, and pick the account under Paid with.
- If it looks like one of your bills, choose Record the amount on this bill.
- Or choose File without attaching, and attach it later when the statement arrives.

Tick Keep this document for warranties and purchase receipts you want to keep for good. Read with AI can fill in the details, if AI reading is set up.

## Find and clean up

All documents lets you search the text and filter by date and amount. Save a copy… exports a file. Old documents lists filed documents older than the six years the Canada Revenue Agency asks you to keep tax records. Nothing is deleted automatically, and documents marked to keep never appear there.

## E-receipts from email

Save an emailed receipt from your email program as a file (.eml) and import it, drop it here or put it in the watched folder. Its PDF or picture attachments come in as documents; an email without attachments is kept as a PDF of its text, and its merchant, date and total are read from it. RANN's Roost never signs in to your mailbox.
