# Security

Security holds the locking setting for this computer. You find it in the Settings group of the menu.

Your household is stored encrypted on this computer. It opens only with a user's password or recovery key, and there is no server that can reset a password for you.

## Lock after inactivity

Choose how long the app waits before locking itself: 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or Never. After that long without keyboard or mouse activity, the household locks and its keys are wiped from memory. You then sign in again to continue.

This setting applies to this computer only. On a computer that others can use, a short delay is safer.

## Lock right away

To lock the household yourself, for example when you step away, click Lock at the top of the window. Closing the window also locks it.

## Keep your recovery key safe

Each user receives a recovery key when their account is made. Keep the printed key somewhere safe, away from this computer. It is the only way back in if a password is forgotten. On the unlock screen, Forgot your password? lets you set a new password with that key.

To change your password, go to Users and click Change my password…. Your recovery key stays the same.
