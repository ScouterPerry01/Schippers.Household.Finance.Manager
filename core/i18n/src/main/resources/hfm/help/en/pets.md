# Pets

Pets keeps a card for each animal in the household, with its details, papers and what it costs. It is in the Home and family group of the menu.

## Add a pet

Choose Add a pet. Enter the name, the animal, breed, colour, sex and date of birth (tick Estimated if you are not sure). You can also note:

- whether it is spayed or neutered
- the microchip number
- the municipal licence, with its number, municipality and expiry date
- pet insurance, with the insurer, policy number and renewal date
- the owner, a person or the whole household

When a pet is no longer in the household, edit it and tick No longer in the household. Its card is then hidden; tick Show former pets to see it again. Deleting a pet keeps the transactions recorded for it.

## Health records and appointments

- Choose Health records to open the Health section for this pet. There you can record its medications, vaccines, tests, allergies and conditions, as for a person.
- Choose Add appointment to put a vet visit or grooming on the calendar.

## Costs

Each card shows what the pet cost this year and over the last 12 months. Choose Costs for more detail. Costs come from the register: when you enter a transaction, choose the pet under "For".
