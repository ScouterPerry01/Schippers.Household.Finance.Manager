# Medical claims

Medical claims follows each medical or dental expense, from the receipt to what the household paid itself. It is in the Home and family group of the menu. The app does not give medical or tax advice. Check your benefit booklet and the CRA or Revenu Québec rules.

## Set up your plans

On the Plans tab, choose Add a plan. Add your employer's health and dental plans, a Health Spending Account, RAMQ or the Canadian Dental Care Plan. For each plan, say who it covers and in what order it pays for each person: first for the plan member and their children, second for a spouse who has a plan of their own. Choose Add coverage to enter what the plan pays for each kind of care.

## Record an expense

- On the Expenses and claims tab, choose Add an expense. Enter the person treated, the kind of care, the date and the cost.
- Tick Counts for the medical expense tax credit when it applies.
- Attach the receipt with Attach a file…, or pick a scan From the review inbox.
- If health payments in your accounts are not yet recorded here, a From the books button lists them so you can add each one.

## Follow the claims

The app shows which plan to send each expense to next, and about how much should come back. Choose Send to (the plan's name) when you send the claim. When the plan answers, choose Record the payment, or Refused if the claim was turned down. Choose Close: nothing more to claim when you are done.

## Coverage left

The Coverage left tab shows what is left of each plan's yearly maximums this plan year, and when a limited service is covered again.
