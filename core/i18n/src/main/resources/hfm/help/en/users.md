# Users

Users is where you decide who can sign in to the household and what each person can open. You find it in the Settings group of the menu.

Each person signs in with their own name and password.

## Roles

- Administrator: everything, including users and settings, and every shared account group.
- Member: their own private accounts, and the shared groups they are given.
- Viewer: read only, in the groups they are given.

## Add a user

Only an administrator can add users. Click Add a user, then enter the name, the login name, the role and, if it applies, which household member this user is. Type a new password twice. A passphrase of a few words is easy to remember and hard to guess.

When you save, the app shows a recovery key for the new user. Give it to them to print or write down. It is the only way back in if the password is forgotten, and it is not shown again.

## Change your password

Click Change my password…, type your current password, then the new one twice. Your recovery key stays the same.

## Give access to account groups

On the Access tab, choose for each person and each account group: No access, View, Capture only or Edit. Capture only lets a person add receipts and transactions without changing anything else. Administrators always open shared groups, and the owner of a private group always has it.

## See who did what

The Activity tab lists changes made in the household. Administrators can pick whose activity to show; others see their own.

To stop someone from signing in without deleting them, edit the user and clear Can sign in.
