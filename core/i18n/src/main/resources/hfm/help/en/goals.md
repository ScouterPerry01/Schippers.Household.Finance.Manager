# Savings goals

Savings goals help you set money aside for specific things, such as a trip, a car or an emergency fund. The money stays in an account you already have. Nothing moves between accounts: each goal earmarks part of the balance.

It is in the Money group of the menu. Goals are grouped by account. Each account shows its balance, the part set aside and the part not assigned. If the goals add up to more than the balance, a warning tells you to take some back from a goal or deposit more.

## Add a goal

Choose Add a goal. Enter what you are saving for, the account it is kept in, the target amount and, if you like, a target date. To save on a schedule, tick Set an amount aside on a schedule and enter the amount each time, how it repeats and the first date. Tick Enter the set-asides automatically on their dates.

Each goal shows its progress. With a target date, it tells you whether you are on track, or how much to set aside to catch up.

## Set money aside or use it

- Set aside earmarks more of the account's balance for the goal. No money moves.
- Use records that the goal paid for what it was saved for, or, under Reason, that money was taken back for something else. Enter the purchase itself in the account as usual.
- Move shifts an amount to another goal in the same account.

## History and changes

History lists every set-aside, use and move for a goal; you can delete an entry there. Edit changes the goal, and Delete removes it with its history. The money stays in the account.
