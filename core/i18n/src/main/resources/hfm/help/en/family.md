# Family money

Family money keeps track of money between people. It is in the Money group of the menu, and has four tabs.

## Shared expenses

For a trip, a cottage weekend or a shared apartment, choose New shared group… and name the people, from the household or not. Then Add an expense… for each bill: what it was for, the amount, who paid, and the shares. One share each splits it equally; give 2 shares to someone who counts double, such as a couple, or 0 to leave someone out.

The cards show who is owed and who owes. To settle up lists the fewest payments that make everyone even; Mark paid records one. When everything is settled, Edit the group… can archive it.

## Family loans

Add a family loan… records money lent between family members or friends: who lent, to whom, how much, when, and an interest rate if there is one. The interest is simple, on what is still owed, and each repayment goes to the interest first. Click a loan to add repayments and see what is still owed.

The CRA has rules on low-interest loans between spouses; ask an advisor before setting one up. RANN's Roost does not give tax advice.

## Allowances

Set up an allowance… for a child: the amount, how often and the first day. Each allowance day adds to what is owed until Mark paid records it. Click an allowance to record money the child earned or received, or spent: the child's balance is what they have. Mark paid also pays the chores done and not paid yet.

## Chores

Add a chore for a child, worth an amount, points, or both. Done today ticks it; the child or a parent can also tick it on the phone (Chores on the Capture tab). Each child shows what is still to pay; Pay with the allowance adds it to the child's money as earned, and the points add up.
