# Payees

Payees are the people and businesses you pay or get paid by: the grocery store, Hydro-Québec, your employer. Keeping a clean list makes transactions easier to read and to search. Payees are in Settings, under Payees.

## Add a payee

- Click Add.
- Type the Payee name.
- Optionally pick a Default category. When you enter a transaction for this payee, or import one from a statement, this category is suggested.
- Click Save.

## Teach the app how a payee looks on statements

Banks often print names in a short or odd form, such as AMZN MKTP. An alias links that text to the right payee.

- Select the payee in the list.
- Type the text in Alias, for example AMZN MKTP.
- Click Add alias.

When an imported line contains an alias, ignoring upper and lower case, it gets this payee's clean name.

## Change or hide a payee

- Select a payee to change its name or default category, then click Save.
- Tick Archived (hidden from lists) for a payee you no longer use. Past transactions keep their payee.

For finer control, such as choosing a category by amount, use Category rules.
