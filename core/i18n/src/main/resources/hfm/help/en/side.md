# Side income

Side income is for money earned outside a job: tutoring, crafts, small contracts, or a rental property. It is in the Money group of the menu, and has two tabs.

## Invoices

New invoice starts the next number for the year, such as 2026-001. Enter the customer, the dates and the lines: a description, a quantity and a price for each. If you are registered to collect sales taxes, enter the GST or HST rate and, in Quebec, the QST rate, or the PST rate elsewhere; otherwise leave them empty.

PDF saves the invoice to send. When the customer pays, Mark as paid can also record the deposit in a bank account, as self-employment income with the sales taxes collected noted on it. An invoice sent and past its due date shows Overdue.

## Rental properties

Add a property creates a tag with its name. Put that tag on the property's rent and expenses in the registers, and the tab adds them up by category for the year you choose: income, expenses and net. If the household owns only part of the property, enter its share, and the tab shows the household's part of the net.

RANN's Roost does not prepare a T776 or give tax advice; the totals help you or your preparer fill one in.
