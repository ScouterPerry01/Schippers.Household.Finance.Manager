# Household members

Household members are the people in your household: adults, children and other dependants. They are used to say whose account, health record or claim something is. Household members are in Settings, the first item of that group.

Household members are not the same as Users. A user is someone who signs in to the app; a member is a person the records are about. A child can be a member without ever signing in.

## Set the province or territory

At the top of the screen, choose the household's Province or territory. Its rules apply: bank holidays for bills, default categories, provincial RESP grants, locked-in plans and tax forms. Only an administrator can change it. An administrator can also change the Household name there and click Rename.

## Add a person

- Click Add.
- Type the Name.
- Choose the Relationship: Adult, Child or Other dependant.
- Optionally enter the Date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD). It is used, for example, for ages in registered plans.
- Under Lives in, keep Same as the household, or choose another province for someone who lives elsewhere, such as a student away at school.
- Click Save.

## Change or hide a person

Select a person in the list, change what you need, then click Save. Tick Archived (hidden from lists) for someone who is no longer part of the household; their past records stay.
