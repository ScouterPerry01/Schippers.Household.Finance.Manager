# Walk-Me guides

A Walk-Me guide takes you through a common task step by step, in a panel on the right of the window that does not block the app. The guides are grouped: Getting started; Bills and budgets; Documents and the phone; Borrowing and investing; Home, family and vehicles; Taxes and reports.

## Start a guide

Click Start beside a guide. A guide you closed before its end offers Resume at step, or Start over. Before any household is open, the welcome screen's Walk-Me guides button shows the list, starting with Create your household.

## Follow the steps

- Each step says where to go, what to choose and what to type, with the labels as on the screen.
- Show me opens the screen and tab the step is about. The control to use is outlined on the screen.
- Many steps move on by themselves once the app sees them done, such as a dialog opening or a record added. Back and Next move between steps; Finish ends the guide.
- Read more in the manual opens the manual on that subject.
- Steps done on the phone say what to tap there; press Next when they are done.

Drag the panel's left edge to resize it, or Minimize it to a narrow strip. Each guide's progress is kept on this computer.
