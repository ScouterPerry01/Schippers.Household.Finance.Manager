# Loans and mortgages

Loans and mortgages shows the terms, payment schedule, prepayments, renewals and what-ifs for each loan and mortgage account. It is in the Investing and borrowing group of the menu.

The loan itself is an account. Add it under Accounts, with what you owe as a negative opening balance. Then choose it in the list on this screen.

## Enter the terms

Click Enter terms and fill in the principal, rate, payment frequency, first payment and, for a mortgage, the term end (renewal date). For a mortgage that has been renewed, enter the balance, rate and remaining amortization at the start of the current term. Property tax and insurance collected with each payment can be entered too.

Once the terms are in, the screen shows what is owed, the next payment, the payoff date, the interest still to pay and when the term ends.

## Record payments and changes

- Record payment moves the whole amount out of the paying account as one transfer. Interest, property tax and insurance go to their categories, so only the principal reduces what you owe.
- Prepayment records a lump sum paid off the principal.
- Renew, Rate change and Payment change record a new term, a new rate or a new payment amount.

The Changes tab lists these changes; you can delete one there.

## See the schedule

The By year and Every payment tabs show the payment schedule. You can export it to CSV, Excel or PDF, or Print it.

## What if…

Click What if… to try an extra amount each payment, a lump sum now, or another rate or amortization. The app shows the interest you would save, starting from what is owed today. Nothing is changed in your books.
