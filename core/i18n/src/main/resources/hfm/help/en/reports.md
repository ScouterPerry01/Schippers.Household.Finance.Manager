# Reports

Reports turns your books into charts and tables. It is in the Reports and taxes group of the menu.

The list on the left holds every report: Income and expense, Spending by category, Income by category, Spending by payee, Custom report, Year in review, Net worth, Investment portfolio, Investment income and capital gains, Registered plans, Medical expenses, Assets and warranties, Debt summary, Budget vs actual, Reconciliation status and others.

## Run a report

- Click a report in the list.
- Choose the Period above the chart, such as this month or last year, or set your own dates. Some reports ask for a year instead.
- Narrow it down with Choose accounts…, or by person or tag where the report offers it.
- For income and spending reports, use Compare with to set this period beside the previous period or the same period last year.

## Look at the numbers

Under the chart, click Show table to see the figures. The buttons beside it export the report to CSV, Excel or PDF, or Print it.

## Build your own

Custom report lets you choose what the rows and columns are, and whether it adds up spending, income, or income less spending. Year in review sums up a year from the books.

## Save a report

Click Save this report… to keep the current choices under a name. Saved reports appear under Saved reports at the bottom of the list; click the ✕ beside one to remove it.

A custom report can also be made on a schedule: each month, each quarter or each year. The PDF goes to a folder you choose. It is made the first time the household is open after the period ends, and missed periods are made then.
