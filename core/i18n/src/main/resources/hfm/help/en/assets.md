# Home and assets

Home and assets keeps track of the home and everything worth keeping track of: where it is, what it cost and is worth, its warranties, maintenance and insurance. Vehicles have their own section. It is in the Home and family group of the menu.

## Add an asset

On the Assets tab, choose Add an asset. Enter the kind, name, make, model, serial number and location. Use Find the purchase to link the purchase from your accounts. Under Value, enter an estimate or let the value fall each month from the price. Tick Count in net worth if it should count. Add warranties with Add a warranty, and photos under Photos and documents.

## Maintenance

Inside an asset, add tasks with Add usual tasks or Add a task. A task repeats after a number of months, hours or kilometres on its meter, or every year in its season, whichever comes first. The Maintenance tab lists what is due on vehicles and everything else, for This month or the Next 12 months.

## Is it covered?

On the Is it covered? tab, type in Find an item to search by name, make, model or serial number. You see right away whether it is under warranty or protection. To record a warranty claim, open the warranty on the asset and choose Add a claim.

## Insurance

On the Insurance tab, choose Add a policy for home, life, disability or other cover. Note the premium, renewal date and beneficiaries. At renewal time, choose Renew to enter the new premium. The tab also lists assets and vehicles no active policy covers.

For a home inventory with photos, see the Assets and warranties report under Reports.
