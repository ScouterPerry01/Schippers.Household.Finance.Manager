# Categories

Categories sort your income and spending, so budgets and reports can add them up. The list is in Settings, under Categories. Subcategories are shown indented under their parent, and archived categories are greyed out.

## Add a category

- Click New expense category or New income category for a top-level category.
- To add one inside another, select the parent in the list, then click New subcategory.
- Type the Name in English and the Name in French. Both names are kept, so the household can switch languages.
- Click Save.

## Mark a category for taxes

Use Tax treatment to link a category to a tax line, such as Medical expenses, Charitable donations, Political contributions, Child care expenses, Tuition, Self-employment, Employment expenses or Moving expenses. The list shows this tag next to the category's name. A new subcategory starts with its parent's tax treatment. This helps you gather amounts at tax time; it is not tax advice, so check what you can claim with the CRA or Revenu Québec.

## Change or hide a category

- Select a category in the list to edit its names or tax treatment, then click Save.
- Tick Archived (hidden from lists) to stop seeing a category you no longer use. Past transactions keep it.

The household's province or territory, set on Household members, decides the default categories a new household starts with.
