# Trip log

The trip log keeps the trips you make by car for work or for medical care. It is in the Home and family group of the menu.

## Log a trip

Choose Add a trip and enter the date, where you went, the kilometres one way and whether it was a round trip. Choose the purpose (business, employment, medical or personal), the person and the vehicle. Click a trip to change or delete it.

## Trips from the phone

A trip started on the phone can have several stops and rest breaks. Each stop gives a leg with its own distance and purpose, shown under the trip and as its own line in the logbook; breaks are left out of the driving time. Photos and notes taken on the way open in Documents.

## A vehicle's work share

The cards at the top add up each person's kilometres by purpose. For a vehicle, they also compare the business and employment kilometres with all the kilometres it was driven in the year. That total comes from its odometer readings on the Vehicles screen: add one near the start of the year and one near the end. The work share is what the CRA asks for when you claim vehicle expenses.

## Medical travel

When care is not available near home and the trip is 40 km or more one way, it can count as a medical expense. Such a trip shows Add to medical expenses. Choose the person and enter the rate per kilometre the CRA publishes for your province that year; RANN's Roost keeps the rate for the next trips and adds the trip to Medical expenses.

RANN's Roost does not give tax advice; check the CRA's rules before claiming.
