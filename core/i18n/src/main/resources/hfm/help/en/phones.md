# Phones

On this screen you pair the RANN's Roost Mobile app on your Android phone with this computer. Paired phones send receipts, bills and quick expenses to the Documents inbox, where you review them. They also send odometer readings, trips, fill-ups, saved places, meter and tank readings, hours worked, chores, volunteer hours and seasonal tasks done, which are recorded straight on their screens, new contacts for review on Contacts, and the calendars their owner chooses. The screen is in Settings, under Phones.

Everything is encrypted between the phone and this computer, and over Wi-Fi nothing goes through the Internet. Phones can reach this computer only while the household is open.

## Pair a phone

- Click Pair a phone. A QR code appears.
- On the phone, open RANN's Roost Mobile, tap Pair with a computer, and scan the code. The phone's camera app also works.
- The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer. If Windows asks whether to allow the app on networks, allow it on private networks.
- The code expires after 10 minutes. If it does, close the window and pair again. If the camera cannot read the screen, click Copy as text and paste the link on the phone.

## Away from home

When the phone is not on your Wi-Fi, it can leave its captures, encrypted, in a folder of your own Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or Nextcloud. Click Choose a transfer folder… and pick the same folder as it appears on this computer. New captures are imported from it. The service only sees files it cannot read. A transfer file sent by email or copied by USB can be brought in with Import a transfer file….

## Manage phones

Each phone shows when it was paired, its last transfer and how many items it sent. Click Edit to rename it or choose the account group its documents, trips and log entries go to. Click Remove to stop a lost or old phone; then Forget clears it from the list.

## What the phone receives

After each transfer the phone gets a summary of what its owner can see here: balances, bills due, budgets, maintenance, appointments, work and school hours, refills, renewals, the season's checklist, the lists its forms pick from, and the contacts. It shows them on its Summary tab and in its agenda, and reminds you of them.
