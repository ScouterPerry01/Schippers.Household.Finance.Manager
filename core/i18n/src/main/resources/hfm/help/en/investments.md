# Investments

Investments shows what you hold in your brokerage accounts and registered plans, what it is worth, and your capital gains. It is in the Investing and borrowing group of the menu.

The list on the left shows non-registered accounts first, then registered plans, each with its value. Investment accounts are created under Accounts. Below the list are Capital gains and ACB, Securities and Import from an exchange….

## Record what happened

- Choose an account, then click Add transaction to record a buy, sell, income, reinvested income (DRIP), return of capital, split, merger, fee or units moved in or out.
- Click Import statement… to read a brokerage statement (OFX, QFX or CSV). Transactions already there are not added twice.
- A PDF statement or trade confirmation read by AI goes into the account from its document, in Documents.
- Click Update prices to enter the latest price of each security. Without a price, a security counts at its book cost.

Each account has three tabs: Holdings, Transactions and Statements.

## Check a statement

Click Check a statement and enter the cash and units shown on your brokerage statement. The fields start with the figures in the books. If everything matches, click Mark reconciled.

## Capital gains and ACB

This view shows the adjusted cost base of each security, pooled across non-registered accounts with the same owners, and the year's gains and losses with the taxable half. Registered plans are not included. These figures help prepare Schedule 3; they are not tax advice.

## Crypto and precious metals

A crypto wallet account has its own buttons, such as Buy, Sell and Convert. A precious metals account lists your coins and bars; click Add coins or bars.
