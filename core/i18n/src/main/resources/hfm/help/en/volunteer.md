# Volunteer hours

Volunteer hours keeps the time each person gives to an organization, with a total for the year. It is in the Home and family group of the menu.

## Add volunteer hours

Choose the person, the organization (or a contact that is one), the kind, the date and the time, as 1:30 or 1.5. The kinds are volunteer firefighter, search and rescue, community hours for school, and other volunteering. Click a line to change or delete it. Hours can also be entered on the phone (Volunteer hours on the Capture tab).

## Totals

A card per person shows the year's total, by organization. Volunteer firefighting and search and rescue count together towards the 200 hours of eligible service that the volunteer firefighters amount (line 31220) and the search and rescue volunteers amount (line 31240) need; the card shows how many hours are left. Students' community hours have their own total. The year-end tax package on the Taxes screen shows the totals as information.
