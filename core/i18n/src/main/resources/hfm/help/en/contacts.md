# Contacts

Contacts keeps the people and organizations your household deals with: banks, investment firms, advisors, insurers, doctors, pharmacies, lawyers, contractors, schools and more. It stands on its own in the menu, just under Dashboard. Each contact says what it is for and whom it serves, so you can tell your two banks or your three doctors apart.

## Add a contact

- Choose Add a contact. Enter the Name and, under What for, a few words to tell it apart, such as "RRSP and TFSA" or "Sam's dermatologist". This line shows wherever the contact is named or picked.
- Choose Organization or Person. A person can work at an organization contact (Works at), and is then listed under it.
- Tick one or more Kinds (bank, pharmacy, family doctor...). More kinds… shows the full list.
- Under For whom, tick the people or pets it serves. None ticked means the whole household.
- Add phones, emails and account or client numbers, each with a label such as Office or Cell. Numbers are shown masked; Show number asks for your password.
- Store in chooses the account group. Choose a private group to keep a contact to yourself.

## Find a contact

Use the search box (accents and capitals do not matter) and the filters: Kind, For whom and Linked to. For example, Kind Bank lists all your banks, and For whom Léa lists everyone who looks after Léa. The search at the top of the window finds contacts too.

## Links to your records

A contact's page lists every record it is linked to, grouped by role: Bank for, Lender for, Insurer for, Pharmacy for, Executor for... Click a record to open its screen. Link to a record… adds a link.

The records' own screens show their contacts too, with a Link a contact… button: accounts, loans, investments, insurance policies, health providers, medications, appointments, bills, payees, institutions, pets, vehicles, assets, contractors and estate papers.

## Gather contacts from the app

The first time you open Contacts, and whenever you choose Gather contacts from the app, the app offers to make contacts from the institutions, health providers, contractors, insurers, brokers and estate contacts it already holds, each linked to where it came from. Records that look like the same contact (same name or phone) are shown together; tick Make them one contact to merge them. Nothing is moved or deleted.
