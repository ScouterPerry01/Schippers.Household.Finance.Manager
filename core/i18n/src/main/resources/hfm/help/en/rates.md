# Rates and prices

This screen keeps the exchange rates and market prices the app uses to value things in Canadian dollars. It is in Settings, under Rates and prices. Every download here is optional, and rates or prices you enter yourself are never replaced.

## Exchange rates

Amounts in other currencies are converted at the Bank of Canada's daily rate. Each currency you use is listed with its latest rate, date and source.

- Click Update rates to download the latest rates from the Bank of Canada.
- Click Recent rates next to a currency to see its rates for the last weeks. A rate entered by hand can be deleted there.
- Under Follow other currencies, pick a Currency and click Follow to get rates for a currency you have no account in, for travel or family abroad. Click Stop following to drop it.

## Currencies the Bank of Canada does not publish

The Bank of Canada publishes 27 currencies. Under Second source of rates (optional), tick Also download rates the Bank of Canada does not publish to get the others from ExchangeRate-API. Only the list of rates is downloaded; nothing about your household is sent.

## Enter a rate yourself

Under Enter a rate yourself, type the Currency code, the date and the Canadian dollars per unit, then click Save.

## Market prices

Under Market prices (optional), each download is off until you tick it: stock and ETF prices from Yahoo Finance, crypto-asset prices from CoinGecko, and precious metal prices. Turning one on sends the symbols of what you hold to that service, but nothing else. Then click Update prices now. You can also enter a metal's price per troy ounce by hand and click Save.
