# Calendar

The Calendar brings together your appointments and the dates the rest of the app already knows. It is in the Money group of the menu.

Besides the appointments you add, it shows:

- Bills coming due, from Bills.
- Health items that are due, from Health.
- Renewals, such as insurance, registrations, warranties ending or a loan term ending.
- Maintenance due on your vehicles and your home and assets.

Each of these has a button, such as Open bills or Open health, that takes you to the screen where it is managed.

## Agenda and Month

The Agenda tab lists what is coming in the next 60 days, by day. The Month tab shows a whole month. On the month view, click a day to add an appointment, and click an appointment to change it.

## Add an appointment

Choose Add an appointment. Enter what it is, the kind, the date and the time, or tick All day. You can also enter the length in minutes, where it is, who it is for, a provider and a related account. Set how it repeats if it comes back regularly. Under Remind me, choose when you want a reminder, for example when it starts or a day before. Store in lets you keep it in a shared group or in your private group, so only you can see it.

## Mark appointments

- Done marks this occurrence as done.
- Cancel this one cancels only this occurrence; the other repeats stay.
- Undo puts it back as it was.
- Edit opens the appointment to change or delete it. Deleting removes all its repeats.

## Calendars from phones and .ics files

Items brought in from a person's phone show read-only, with the calendar they come from and whose phone sent them; someone else's busy-only items show as "Alex: busy". The calendars are chosen on the phone, in Settings, Calendars on this phone. Phone calendars lists yours and lets you choose the group where others see them. Import .ics file copies a calendar file once, as ordinary appointments.
