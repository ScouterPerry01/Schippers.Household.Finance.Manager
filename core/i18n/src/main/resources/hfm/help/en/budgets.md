# Budgets

Budgets compares what you plan to spend in each category with what you actually spend. It is in the Money group of the menu.

Each line shows a category with the amount spent (or received, for income), the amount budgeted, and how much is left or by how much it is over. Use the arrows at the top to move from month to month. Tick Whole year to see the full year instead of one month.

## Let the app suggest budgets

If you already have some history, choose Suggest from last 12 months. The app shows the monthly average of each category over the last 12 full months. Tick the categories you want to budget, then choose Create budgets. Categories that already have a budget are marked. Only shared accounts count, since every user of the household sees the budgets.

## Add a budget by hand

Choose Add a budget. Pick the category and enter the amount. Under Budget is, choose Monthly or Yearly. A yearly budget suits costs that come once or twice a year, such as insurance or property tax. Enter the first month the budget applies to. A budget on a category covers its subcategories too, unless they have a budget of their own.

## Carry amounts over

Tick Carry unspent or overspent amounts into the next month if you want what is left, or what was overspent, to roll into the following month. The line then notes how much was carried over.

## Change a budget

Click a line to change its amount or settings, or to delete it.

The Dashboard shows this month's spending against your budgets and how many categories are over budget. A budget report is also available in Reports.
