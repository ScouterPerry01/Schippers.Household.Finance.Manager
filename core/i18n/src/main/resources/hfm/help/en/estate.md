# Emergency and estate

Emergency and estate gathers what a spouse or an executor would need: where each person's papers are, who to call, and every institution, account, policy and plan. It is in the Home and family group of the menu. The app does not give legal advice. For a will, a mandate or a power of attorney, see a notary or a lawyer.

## Papers and wishes

On the Papers and wishes tab, choose a person and note where things are, not the papers themselves:

- where the will is, and its date
- the power of attorney for property, and the mandate in case of incapacity or personal care directive (in Quebec, the protection mandate)
- the safe deposit box and where the keys are
- where the list of online accounts or the password manager's recovery is kept; never a password itself
- other papers, organ and tissue donor wishes, and funeral wishes

Choose Add a person to list the people to call. Choose Keep it in to pick the group it is kept in; a private group keeps it from other household users. Choose Save. Once saved, you can attach scans of the will, mandate or contracts.

## Emergency summary

The Emergency summary tab puts it all together: each person's papers and contacts, then the household's institutions, accounts with their balances and beneficiaries, insurance policies, pensions and documents kept for good.

## Hand it over

- Choose Save as PDF… to save the summary. Leave Protect it with a password ticked, and give the password separately, by phone or in person. There is no way to open the file without it.
- Choose Print for a paper copy.

Keep any copy somewhere safe.
