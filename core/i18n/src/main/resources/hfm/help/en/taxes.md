# Taxes

Taxes gathers the tax year in one place: the slips to expect, donations and their official receipts, instalments, and the package for the return. It is in the Reports and taxes group of the menu.

The figures here are organizational aids, not tax advice. Check them against your slips and statements, and ask a tax professional when in doubt.

## Slips

The Slips tab lists the slips each person should receive, worked out from the books: income by payer, plan withdrawals and contributions, investment income and sales. Most arrive by the end of February; T3 and RL-16 slips by March 31.

- Click a slip to set its Status to Expected, Received or Not expected, and to attach the slip itself.
- Click Add a slip… for one the books cannot predict.

## Donations

The Donations tab shows every payment on a charitable or political category, by the person who gave it. Click a gift to enter its official receipt: the charity, the receipt number and the eligible amount. A warning shows how many receipts are missing.

## Instalments

Click Set up instalments… and enter the amounts from the CRA or Revenu Québec reminder. Instalments are due March 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15. Payments recorded on the Tax instalments category count towards them.

## Year-end package

The Year-end package tab shows each person's figures for the return, gathered from the books, with the federal line each one goes on. Choose the year and the person. Click Folder for the accountant… to save a folder with every summary and the slips and receipts filed for them, or export one summary as PDF, Excel or CSV.
