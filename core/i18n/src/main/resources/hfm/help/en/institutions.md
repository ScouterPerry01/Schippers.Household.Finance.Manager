# Institutions

Institutions are the banks, credit unions, brokers and other companies that hold your accounts. Recording them once lets you link each account to its institution and keep its contact details at hand. Institutions are in Settings, under Institutions.

## Add an institution

- Click Add.
- Type the Name, for example Desjardins or TD Canada Trust.
- Optionally add the Branch, the Institution number (3 digits) and the Transit number (5 digits). These are the same numbers printed on your cheques and on a direct deposit form.
- Optionally add the Website, the Phone and any Notes.
- Click Save.

## Change an institution

Select it in the list, change what you need, then click Save. The change shows everywhere the institution is used.

## Why it helps

- When you add an account under Accounts, you can choose its institution.
- The phone number and website are handy when a card is lost or a payment needs to be stopped.
- The Emergency and estate summary can list where the household's accounts are held, which helps someone who has to take over.

Account numbers themselves are kept with each account, not here.
