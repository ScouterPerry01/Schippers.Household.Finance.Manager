# Keyboard shortcuts

RANN's Roost can be used with the mouse or the keyboard. These are the shortcuts the app adds to the usual ones, such as Tab to move between fields.

## Anywhere in the household

- F1: open this help.
- Ctrl+F: go to the search box at the top of the window.

## In the search box

- Enter: search. Type at least two characters.

The results are grouped by kind: transactions, accounts, payees, categories, bills, institutions and documents. Click a result to open it where it lives.

## In an account's register

The form below the transactions, where you enter or edit a transaction, answers to two keys:

- Enter: save the transaction.
- Escape: clear the form without saving.

## In forms that open in a window

- Escape: close the window without saving, the same as Cancel.

## Tips

- Lock the household with the Lock button at the top of the window when you step away. Security sets how soon it locks by itself.
- The menu can sit on the left or at the top. Use Menu at the top or Menu on the left to switch.
