# Family money and allowances
@about: Split shared costs, follow money lent within the family, and pay a child's allowance.

## Open Family money {#open}
@screen: FAMILY FamilyTab.SHARED
@done: screen
@manual: family#screen

In the menu, open **Money**, then **Family money**. It has four tabs: **Shared expenses**, **Family loans**, **Allowances** and **Chores**.

## Share costs {#shared}
@screen: FAMILY FamilyTab.SHARED
@target: family.newGroup
@manual: family#shared-expenses

For a trip, a cottage weekend or a shared apartment, click **New shared group…** and name the people who share, in the household or not.

## Add what each paid {#expense}
@screen: FAMILY FamilyTab.SHARED
@target: family.addExpense
@manual: family#expense-dialog

Click **Add an expense…**: what it was for, the **Amount**, who it was **Paid by**, and the **Shares**: 1 each to split equally, 2 for someone who counts double, 0 to leave someone out. **To settle up** then lists the fewest payments that make everyone even.

## Money lent in the family {#loans}
@screen: FAMILY FamilyTab.LOANS
@target: family.addLoan
@manual: family#loan-dialog

On the **Family loans** tab, click **Add a family loan…**: **Lent by**, **Lent to**, the **Amount lent**, the **Date lent** and, if any, the **Interest rate (% a year)**. Record each repayment on the loan as it comes. Ask an advisor about the CRA's rules on low-interest loans within a family.

## Set up an allowance {#allowance}
@screen: FAMILY FamilyTab.ALLOWANCES
@target: family.addAllowance
@done: shown allowance.dialog
@manual: family#allowance-dialog

On the **Allowances** tab, click **Set up an allowance…**.

## The allowance {#allowance-form}
@target: allowance.dialog.save
@manual: family#allowance-dialog

Choose the **Person**, the **Amount**, **How often** (**a week**, **every two weeks** or **a month**) and the **First day**. Click **Save**. Each allowance day then counts as owed to the child.

## Pay it {#pay}
@screen: FAMILY FamilyTab.ALLOWANCES
@target: family.payAllowance
@manual: family#allowance-list

When you give the child their allowance, click **Mark paid** on its line. Click the allowance to see the child's money, and add what they earned, received or spent. Chores ticked on the **Chores** tab or on the phone are paid with it.
