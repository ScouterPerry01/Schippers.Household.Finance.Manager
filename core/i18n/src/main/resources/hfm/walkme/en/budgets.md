# Budgets
@about: Give your spending categories a monthly or yearly amount, from your history or by hand.

## Open Budgets {#open}
@screen: BUDGETS
@done: screen
@manual: budgets#overview

In the menu, open **Money**, then **Budgets**. Each budget shows how much of it is spent this month or year.

## Start from your history {#suggest}
@screen: BUDGETS
@target: budgets.suggest
@manual: budgets#suggest

With a few months of transactions, click **Suggest from last 12 months**: it proposes a monthly budget for each spending category, the monthly average of the last 12 full months. Tick the ones you want and click **Create budgets**. Then adjust each one by clicking its bar.

New to the app? Skip to the next step and add budgets by hand.

## Add a budget {#add}
@screen: BUDGETS
@target: budgets.add
@done: shown budget.category
@manual: budgets#choose-category

Click **Add a budget**.

## Choose the category {#category}
@target: budget.category.save
@done: shown budget.dialog
@manual: budgets#choose-category

Pick the **Category**, such as Groceries; type to filter the list. A budget covers the subcategories too, unless they have their own. Click **Continue**.

## Set the amount {#amount}
@target: budget.dialog.save
@done: added budget
@manual: budgets#budget-form

- **Budget is**: **Monthly**, or **Yearly** for costs such as insurance.
- **Amount**: for one month, or for the whole year.
- **Carry unspent or overspent amounts into the next month**: tick it to let what is left over, or overspent, roll into the next month.

Click **Save**. The phone shows this month's budgets too, with alerts at 80 % and 100 %.
