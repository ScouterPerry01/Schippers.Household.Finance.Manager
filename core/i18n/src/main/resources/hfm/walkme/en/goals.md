# Savings goals
@about: Earmark part of a savings account for a goal, by hand or on a schedule.

## Open Savings goals {#open}
@screen: GOALS
@done: screen
@manual: goals#overview

A goal earmarks part of an account's balance: nothing moves between accounts. In the menu, open **Money**, then **Savings goals**.

## Add a goal {#add}
@screen: GOALS
@target: goals.add
@done: shown goal.dialog
@manual: goals#goal-form

Click **Add a goal**.

## What you save for {#describe}
@target: goal.dialog
@manual: goals#goal-form

- **What are you saving for?**: such as Emergency fund or Trip to Gaspésie.
- **Kept in account**: the savings account that holds the money. It cannot be changed later.
- **Target amount** and **Target date (optional)**: with a date, the app tells you whether you are on track.

## A regular amount {#schedule}
@target: goal.dialog.save
@done: added goal
@manual: goals#planned-set-asides

With **Set an amount aside on a schedule** ticked, enter the **Amount each time** and how often it **Repeats**, to match your pay or your transfer to savings. With **Enter the set-asides automatically on their dates** ticked, each one is entered on its date while the app is open.

Click **Save**.

## Set aside by hand {#set-aside}
@screen: GOALS
@target: goals.setAside
@manual: goals#set-aside

To earmark more at any time, click **Set aside** on the goal, enter the **Amount**, and click **Save**. When the goal pays for what it was saved for, use **Use** on it.
