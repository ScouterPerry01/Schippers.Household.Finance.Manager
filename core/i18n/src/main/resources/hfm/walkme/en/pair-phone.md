# Pair a phone
@about: Connect RANN's Roost Mobile to this computer, so it can send receipts and bills over your Wi-Fi.

## On the phone: install and lock {#install}
@manual: phone-app#lock

Install RANN's Roost Mobile on your Android phone and open it. It asks you to choose a PIN of 4 to 8 digits that locks the app: type it, tap **OK**, type it again and tap **OK**.

The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.

## Open Phones {#open}
@screen: PHONES
@done: screen
@manual: phones#how-it-works

On this computer, in the menu, open **Settings**, then **Phones**. Phones reach this computer only while the household is open; nothing goes through the internet.

## Show the pairing code {#code}
@screen: PHONES
@target: phones.pair
@done: shown phones.pairing
@manual: phones#pair

Click **Pair a phone**. A QR code appears, valid for 10 minutes. If Windows asks whether to allow RANN's Roost on the network, allow it on private networks.

## On the phone: scan the code {#scan}
@target: phones.pairing
@done: added phone
@manual: phone-app#pair-screen

On the phone, tap **Pair with a computer** on the Capture tab, then **Scan the code**, and point the camera at the QR code on this screen.

If the camera cannot read the screen, click **Copy as text** here, get the text to the phone in a way you trust, and paste it under **Or paste the pairing text**.

## Paired {#paired}
@screen: PHONES
@manual: phones#received

The window says the phone is paired and closes by itself; the phone shows **Paired with** and your household's name. The phone appears in the list here.

Each person can pair their own phone, signed in as themselves. Next, follow the guide Capture and process a receipt.
