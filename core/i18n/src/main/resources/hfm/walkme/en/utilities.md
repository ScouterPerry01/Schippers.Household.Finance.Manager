# Utilities and meters
@about: Follow electricity, gas and water use month by month, and fuel tanks before they run low.

## Open Utilities {#open}
@screen: UTILITIES UtilitiesTab.METERS
@done: screen
@manual: utilities#screen

In the menu, open **Home and family**, then **Utilities**. The **Meters** tab follows electricity, gas and water; the **Fuel tanks** tab follows propane or heating oil.

## Add a meter {#add}
@screen: UTILITIES UtilitiesTab.METERS
@target: meters.add
@done: shown meter.dialog
@manual: utilities#meter-dialog

Click **Add a meter**.

## Describe the meter {#meter}
@target: meter.dialog.save
@manual: utilities#meter-dialog

- **Name**: such as House electricity.
- **Measures**: **Electricity**, **Natural gas** or **Water**.
- **Time-of-use readings**: tick it for an electricity meter with on-peak, mid-peak and off-peak totals.
- **Bill for the cost per unit**: your hydro or gas bill, so each month shows about what it cost.

Click **Save**.

## Enter a reading {#reading}
@screen: UTILITIES UtilitiesTab.METERS
@target: meters.readings
@done: shown meter.reading
@manual: utilities#readings

Click **Readings** on the meter's card.

## The number on the meter {#number}
@target: meter.reading.save
@manual: utilities#readings

Type the **Date** and the reading on the meter, then click **Add the reading**. With two readings or more, each month shows its use, the same month last year, and **Unusual** in red when it used much more.

A bill's statements can carry the readings too: link the meter on the bill under **Meter (Utilities)**.

## From the phone {#phone}
@manual: utilities#phone

On a paired phone, tap **Meter or tank reading** on the Capture tab to send a reading where the meter is.

## Fuel tanks {#tanks}
@screen: UTILITIES UtilitiesTab.TANKS
@target: tanks.add
@manual: utilities#tanks

On the **Fuel tanks** tab, click **Add a tank** for propane or heating oil. With its levels and deliveries, the app works out when to order, and the phone reminds you.
