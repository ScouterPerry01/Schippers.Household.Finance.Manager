# Capture and process a bill
@about: Photograph a bill on the phone or import it, then record its amount on your bill or create the bill from it.

## On the phone: scan the bill {#scan}
@manual: phone-app#capture-tab

On the phone, tap **Bill** on the Capture tab and scan each page. Check the **Store or biller**, the date and the amount it read, then tap **Save**. It is sent to this computer when the household is open.

Without a phone, click **Import files…** on the Documents screen and pick the bill's PDF or photo.

## Open To review {#review}
@screen: DOCUMENTS DocumentsTab.INBOX
@done: screen
@manual: documents#to-review-tab

In the menu, open **Money**, then **Documents**, and the **To review** tab.

## Open the bill {#open}
@screen: DOCUMENTS DocumentsTab.INBOX
@target: documents.review
@done: shown documents.window
@manual: documents#document-window

Click **Review** on the bill's line.

## Check what was read {#details}
@target: documents.window
@manual: documents#document-details

Check that **Kind** is **Bill**, and compare **Store or biller**, **Date** and **Total** with the picture. The due date, your account number with the biller and, on a utility bill, the meter readings are read too.

## Record it on your bill {#record}
@target: documents.recordOnBill
@done: added filed
@manual: documents#record-on-bill

When the app recognizes one of your bills, it says so: click **Record it as this bill's statement**. The amount and due date go on that bill, and its due date moves to the bill's.

If it is another of your bills, use **Attach to a bill** and pick it.

## Or create the bill from it {#create}
@target: documents.createBill
@done: added bill
@manual: documents#record-on-bill

When it matches none of your bills, click **Create a bill from this**. The bill form opens, filled in from the document: check the name, **Paid from** and the schedule, then click **Save**. A tax bill whose instalments were read is set up with **Instalments on set dates**.

## Pay it {#pay}
@screen: BILLS BillsTab.AGENDA
@target: bills.markPaid
@manual: bills#mark-paid

The bill's due date and amount are now on the Bills screen's **To pay** tab, with its reminders. Click **Mark paid** when you pay it.
