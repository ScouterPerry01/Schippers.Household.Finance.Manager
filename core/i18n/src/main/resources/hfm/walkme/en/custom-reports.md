# Custom reports
@about: Build a report of your own, save it, and have it made as a PDF every month.

## Open the custom report {#open}
@screen: REPORTS ReportKind.CUSTOM
@done: screen
@manual: reports#custom-report

In the menu, open **Reports and taxes**, then **Reports**, and click **Custom report** in the list.

## Choose the period {#period}
@screen: REPORTS ReportKind.CUSTOM
@manual: reports#filter-bar

In the filter bar at the top, choose the period, and if you wish the account group, the accounts, the person or the tag. These choices stay while you move between reports.

## Rows and columns {#layout}
@screen: REPORTS ReportKind.CUSTOM
@target: custom.rows
@manual: reports#custom-report

- **Rows**: what each line is, such as **Category group**, **Payee**, **Person** or **Month**.
- **Columns**: **Totals only**, **Month**, **Quarter**, **Year**, **Person** and so on.
- **Adds up**: **Spending**, **Income** or **Income less spending**.
- **Chart**: **Bars**, **Lines**, **Ranked bars** or **Table only**.
- **Category**: one category with its subcategories, such as Food, to see groceries and restaurants by month.

## Save it {#save}
@screen: REPORTS ReportKind.CUSTOM
@target: reports.save
@done: shown reports.saveDialog
@manual: reports#save-dialog

Click **Save this report…**.

## Name and schedule {#schedule}
@target: reports.saveDialog.save
@manual: reports#scheduled-reports

Give it a **Name**. To have it made by itself, choose **Make it on a schedule**, such as each month for the month just ended, and **Choose a folder…** for the PDF. The PDFs are not encrypted: choose a folder only you can read. Click **Save**.

The saved report is listed under the reports, to open again with one click.
