# The seasonal checklist
@about: See everything to do this season on the home, the vehicles and other assets, and tick it off here or on the phone.

## Where the tasks come from {#tasks}
@screen: ASSETS AssetsTab.ASSETS
@manual: assets#usual-tasks

The checklist gathers the maintenance tasks of your vehicles and assets. Add your home, cottage, pool or yard on the **Assets** tab of **Home and assets**, then **Add usual tasks** on each: gutters, furnace filter, outside taps and more. For a vehicle, see the guide Vehicles and maintenance.

## Open the checklist {#open}
@screen: ASSETS AssetsTab.SEASONAL
@done: screen
@manual: assets#seasonal-tab

In the menu, open **Home and family**, then **Home and assets**, and the **Seasonal checklist** tab. The current season is shown first, with how far along it is; the buttons above show the seasons to come.

## Tick a task {#tick}
@screen: ASSETS AssetsTab.SEASONAL
@target: seasonal.tick
@manual: assets#tick-task

When a task is done, tick its box. Check the **Date**, add the **Cost** if you wish, and click **Record as done**. The service goes into the vehicle's or asset's service log and the task's schedule starts over.

## Print it {#print}
@screen: ASSETS AssetsTab.SEASONAL
@target: seasonal.print
@manual: assets#print-checklist

Click **Print** or **Save as PDF…** to bring the list outside, then tick the tasks in the app when you are back.

## On the phone {#phone}
@manual: phone-app#seasonal-form

On a paired phone, tap **Seasonal checklist** on the Capture tab: the same tasks, to tick where they are done. Each tick is sent to this computer like a capture and recorded as done.
