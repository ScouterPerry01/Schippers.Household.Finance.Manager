# The emergency and estate summary
@about: Note where each person's papers are and whom to call, and save the household's emergency summary as a protected PDF.

## Open Emergency and estate {#open}
@screen: ESTATE EstateTab.PAPERS
@done: screen
@manual: estate#overview

In the menu, open **Home and family**, then **Emergency and estate**. Most of the summary fills itself from the rest of the app; only the papers and wishes are typed here.

## Each person's papers {#papers}
@screen: ESTATE EstateTab.PAPERS
@target: estate.will
@manual: estate#papers-fields

On the **Papers and wishes** tab, choose the **Person**. Note where each paper is, not its content: **Where the will is** and the **Date of the will**, the power of attorney, the safe deposit box and its keys, where the list of online accounts is kept.

Never type a password or PIN here: note only where they are kept.

## People to call {#contacts}
@screen: ESTATE EstateTab.PAPERS
@target: estate.addContact
@manual: estate#contacts

Click **Add a person** for each one to call: the **Role**, such as **Executor** or **Notary**, the **Name**, the firm, phone and email.

## Save {#save}
@screen: ESTATE EstateTab.PAPERS
@target: estate.save
@manual: estate#save-scans

Click **Save**. Scans of the papers can then be attached to the record.

## The emergency summary {#summary}
@screen: ESTATE EstateTab.SUMMARY
@done: screen
@manual: estate#summary-contents

The **Emergency summary** tab gathers everything in one document: the papers and wishes, the institutions, every account with its balance and beneficiaries, the insurance policies, health plans and pensions.

## Save it protected {#pdf}
@screen: ESTATE EstateTab.SUMMARY
@target: estate.export
@manual: estate#save-pdf

Click **Save as PDF…**. Keep **Protect it with a password** ticked, type a password of 8 characters or more twice, and click **Save as…**. Give the password separately, by phone or in person. Save a new PDF after each change: a saved PDF does not update itself.
