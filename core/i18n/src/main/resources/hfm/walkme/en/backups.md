# Backups
@about: Choose a safe place for backups, how often they are made, and make the first one.

## Open Backups {#open}
@screen: BACKUPS
@done: screen
@manual: backups#about-backups

A backup is an encrypted copy of the whole household, checked after it is made. In the menu, open **Settings**, then **Backups**.

## Choose the folder {#folder}
@screen: BACKUPS
@target: backups.folder
@manual: backups#settings

Click **Choose folder…** and pick an external drive, a network drive or a cloud folder (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox), not the disk the household is on. The backups stay encrypted there.

Only an administrator can change the backup settings.

## How often {#schedule}
@screen: BACKUPS
@target: backups.frequency
@manual: backups#settings

Choose **Automatic backups**: **Every day** or **Every week**. Backups are made while the household is open; one that was due while the computer was off is made soon after you open it.

**Versions to keep** says how many are kept in the folder; click **Save** after changing it.

## Back up now {#now}
@screen: BACKUPS
@target: backups.now
@done: added backup
@manual: backups#back-up-now

Click **Back up now**. The backup is made, checked, and the oldest beyond the number to keep are deleted. The date of the last good backup shows beside the button.

## Restoring later {#restore}
@manual: backups#restore

To restore, or to move to a new computer: click **Lock** and **Back**, then on the welcome screen click **Restore from a backup…**, pick the backup file and the folder to put the household in. An existing household is never overwritten.
