# Capture and process a receipt
@about: Photograph a receipt on the phone or import it here, then file it with its transaction.

## On the phone: scan the receipt {#scan}
@manual: phone-app#scanning

On the phone, open RANN's Roost Mobile and tap **Receipt** on the Capture tab. The scanner finds the edges of the receipt and straightens it; take more pages for a long receipt.

No phone? Skip to the step Or import it here.

## On the phone: check and save {#form}
@manual: phone-app#capture-form

The phone reads the store, the date and the total. Correct them if needed; **Paid with**, **Category** and **For** are optional. Tap **Save**: it is sent to this computer over your Wi-Fi when the household is open, or waits on the phone until it is.

## Or import it here {#import}
@screen: DOCUMENTS DocumentsTab.INBOX
@target: documents.import
@manual: documents#import-files

On this computer, a photo, a PDF or a saved e-receipt can come in too: click **Import files…** on the Documents screen, or drag the file onto it.

## Open To review {#review}
@screen: DOCUMENTS DocumentsTab.INBOX
@done: screen
@manual: documents#to-review-tab

In the menu, open **Money**, then **Documents**. The **To review** tab lists what is waiting, newest first.

## Open the receipt {#open}
@screen: DOCUMENTS DocumentsTab.INBOX
@target: documents.review
@done: shown documents.window
@manual: documents#document-window

Click **Review** on the receipt's line.

## Check what was read {#details}
@target: documents.window
@manual: documents#document-details

Compare **Store or biller**, **Date** and **Total** with the picture and correct them. The app remembers your corrections for the next receipts from the same store.

## File it {#file}
@target: documents.attach
@done: added filed
@manual: documents#filing

Under File it with:

- **Attach**: when the purchase is already in an account, imported from your bank or card. The receipt is attached to that transaction.
- **New transaction from this document**: for a cash purchase, or to record it before the statement comes. Choose **Paid with**, **Category** and **For**, then **Save**.

The receipt leaves **To review** and stays in the vault, attached to its transaction.
