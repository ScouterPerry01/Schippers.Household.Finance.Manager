# Create your household
@about: Make the household, its first administrator and master password, and save the recovery key.

## Open the creation form {#open-form}
@screen: WELCOME
@target: welcome.create
@done: shown create.name
@manual: quick-start#create

On the welcome screen, click **Create a new household**.

If a household is open, click **Lock** at the top right, then **Back**, to reach the welcome screen first.

## Choose where it is kept {#location}
@target: create.folder
@manual: quick-start#create

Click **Choose folder…** and pick a folder on this computer, such as Documents. The household is made inside it, in a folder of its own named after the household.

## Name the household {#name}
@target: create.name
@manual: members#province

Type the **Household name**, such as The Martins, and choose the **Province or territory** whose rules apply: bank holidays, RESP grants and tax forms. Both can be changed later on the **Household members** screen.

## Say who you are {#you}
@target: create.adminName

Enter **Your name** and a **Login name**, the short name you type to sign in. You become the household's first administrator.

## Choose the master password {#password}
@target: create.password
@manual: quick-start#create

Type a **Master password** that follows the rules shown under it, then type it again in **Confirm master password**.

There is no server that can reset it, so choose one you will remember.

## Create the household {#create}
@target: create.submit
@done: shown recovery.key

Click **Create household**. It takes a few seconds while the household is encrypted.

## Save your recovery key {#recovery-key}
@target: recovery.key
@done: open
@manual: quick-start#recovery-key

The recovery key is the only way back in if you forget the master password.

1. Click **Print** or **Copy**, and keep the key somewhere safe, away from this computer.
2. Click **I have saved my recovery key**.

## Where to go next {#next}
@screen: DASHBOARD
@manual: quick-start#guide

The Dashboard shows a Getting started card with the first things to set up.

Next, open **Walk-Me guides** in the **Help** menu and follow Institutions and accounts, then Members and users.
