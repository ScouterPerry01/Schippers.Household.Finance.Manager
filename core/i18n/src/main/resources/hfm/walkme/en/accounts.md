# Institutions and accounts
@about: Add your bank or credit union, then each account with its opening balance.

## Open Institutions {#institutions}
@screen: INSTITUTIONS
@done: screen
@manual: institutions#about-institutions

Institutions are the banks, credit unions and firms that hold your accounts. In the menu, open **Settings**, then **Institutions**, or press Show me.

## Add an institution {#add-institution}
@screen: INSTITUTIONS
@target: institutions.add
@done: shown institution.name
@manual: institutions#add-institution

Click **Add**.

## Fill in the institution {#institution-form}
@target: institution.save
@done: added institution
@manual: institutions#institution-fields

- **Name**: as you want to see it, such as Desjardins or TD Canada Trust. Required.
- **Institution number (3 digits)** and **Transit number (5 digits)**: optional, printed on your cheques.

Click **Save**. Add each bank the same way.

## Open Accounts {#accounts}
@screen: ACCOUNTS
@done: screen
@manual: accounts#accounts-screen

In the menu, open **Money**, then **Accounts**.

## Add an account {#add-account}
@screen: ACCOUNTS
@target: accounts.add
@done: shown account.dialog
@manual: accounts#account-dialog

Click **Add account**.

## Name and type {#account-type}
@target: account.dialog
@manual: accounts#account-fields

- **Account name**: as you call it, such as Joint chequing or Visa Infinite.
- **Type**: chequing, savings, credit card, line of credit, investment and so on. It cannot be changed later.
- **Currency (e.g. CAD, USD, BTC)**: CAD for Canadian dollars.
- **Institution**: the bank you just added.

## Opening balance and owners {#opening}
@target: account.dialog.save
@done: added account
@manual: accounts#account-fields

- **Opening balance** and **Opening date**: usually the closing balance and date of your last statement. Enter what you owe on a card, line of credit or loan as a negative amount, such as -12500.
- **Owners**: tick who owns the account; both spouses for a joint account.

Click **Save**. The new account's register opens.

## Add the others {#next}
@screen: ACCOUNTS
@manual: accounts#import-statement

Add each account the same way. For a credit card, enter its limit and due dates afterwards with **Card details** above its register.

Then bring in your bank's transactions with the guide Import a statement or a Quicken file.
