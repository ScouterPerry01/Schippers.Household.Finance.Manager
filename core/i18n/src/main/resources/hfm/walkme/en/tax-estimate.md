# The income tax estimate
@about: Estimate a person's federal and provincial tax, the refund or balance owing, and try an RRSP contribution.

## Open the estimate {#open}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.ESTIMATE
@done: screen
@manual: taxes#estimate

In the menu, open **Reports and taxes**, then **Taxes**, and the **Estimate** tab. It is an estimate, not a return and not tax advice: your return and your notice of assessment have the final word.

## Whose tax {#person}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.ESTIMATE
@manual: taxes#estimate

Choose the **Tax year** and the **Person**. Each person files their own return, so each has their own estimate, with the rates of the province or territory they live in.

## The figures used {#figures}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.ESTIMATE
@target: taxes.estimateFigures
@manual: taxes#estimate-figures

Under **Figures used**, each amount says where it comes from: the year-end package, or not in the books. Fill in what is missing, such as income the books do not know. What you enter is kept for this person and year, and never changes a transaction.

## The result {#result}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.ESTIMATE
@target: taxes.estimateResult
@manual: taxes#estimate-result

The box at the top gives the **Federal tax**, the provincial tax, the **Total income tax**, what was deducted and paid in instalments, and the **Balance owing** or **Refund**, with the average and marginal rates.

## Try a change {#what-if}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.ESTIMATE
@target: taxes.estimateFigures
@manual: taxes#estimate-figures

To see what an RRSP contribution would save before the deadline, change the RRSP deduction among the figures and watch the result. **Use the books** beside a changed figure puts the books' amount back.
