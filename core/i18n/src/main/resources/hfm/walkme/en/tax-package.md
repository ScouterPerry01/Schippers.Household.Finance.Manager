# The year-end tax package
@about: Check that every slip has come, then gather each person's figures and documents for the return or the accountant.

## Open Taxes {#open}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.SLIPS
@done: screen
@manual: taxes#taxes-screen

In the menu, open **Reports and taxes**, then **Taxes**. Each tab has its own **Tax year**: it starts on the year being prepared.

## Slips to expect {#slips}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.SLIPS
@manual: taxes#slips

The **Slips** tab lists the slips each person should receive, found from the books: T4 from an employer, T5 from a bank, T3 and RL-16 for funds, and so on. Mark each **Received** as it arrives and attach the slip. Most come by the end of February; T3 and RL-16 by March 31.

## A slip the books cannot know {#add-slip}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.SLIPS
@target: taxes.addSlip
@manual: taxes#add-slip

Click **Add a slip…** for a slip the books cannot know about, such as one from a former employer.

## Donations {#donations}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.DONATIONS
@manual: taxes#donations

The **Donations** tab lists every donation in the books, by person. Attach each official receipt to its gift; the receipts go into the package.

## The package {#package}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.YEAR_END
@target: taxes.package
@manual: taxes#year-end-package

On the **Year-end package** tab, choose the **Tax year** and the **Person**. Each line shows a figure for the return, where it comes from, and the line it goes on: employment income, RRSP deductions, medical expenses, donations, tax deducted and more.

## For the accountant {#folder}
@screen: TAXES TaxesTab.YEAR_END
@target: taxes.packageFolder
@manual: taxes#accountant-folder

Click **Folder for the accountant…** and choose a folder. It makes a folder with each person's summary as PDF and Excel, and a copy of every slip, donation receipt and medical receipt behind the figures.

The **CSV**, **Excel** and **PDF** buttons beside it save the summary of the person shown only.
