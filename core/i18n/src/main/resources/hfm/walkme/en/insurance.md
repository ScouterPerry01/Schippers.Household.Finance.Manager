# Insurance
@about: Record your home, auto and life policies, what each covers, their renewals and claims.

## Open Insurance {#open}
@screen: ASSETS AssetsTab.INSURANCE
@done: screen
@manual: assets#insurance-tab

In the menu, open **Home and family**, then **Home and assets**, and the **Insurance** tab.

## Add a policy {#add}
@screen: ASSETS AssetsTab.INSURANCE
@target: insurance.add
@done: shown insurance.dialog
@manual: assets#policy-form

Click **Add a policy**.

## Describe the policy {#policy}
@target: insurance.dialog
@manual: assets#policy-form

- **Kind**: **Home**, **Tenant**, **Auto**, **Life**, **Disability** and so on.
- **Insurer**, **Policy number** and **Person insured**.
- **Premium** and **Paid**: how much and how often.
- **Renewal date**: you are reminded from 30 days before.
- **What it covers**: tick the home, vehicles and other assets it covers. Covering a home covers everything that is part of it.

Click **Save**. A life policy then takes its beneficiaries.

## At renewal {#renew}
@target: insurance.renew
@manual: assets#renew

Open the policy. Under **Premiums year over year**, enter the **Next renewal date** and the **New premium**, and click **Renew**. The list shows how the premium changed year over year.

## Making a claim {#claim}
@target: insurance.addClaim
@manual: assets#insurance-claims

In the policy, click **Add a claim**: the date, what happened, the item, and the amounts claimed and paid. Attach photos of the damage and the insurer's letters to it.

## What is not insured {#uninsured}
@screen: ASSETS AssetsTab.INSURANCE
@target: insurance.uninsured
@manual: assets#uninsured

Under **Not covered by any policy**, the assets and vehicles no active policy covers are listed in red with their value. Tick them under **What it covers** in the right policy, or decide not to insure them.
