# Contacts
@about: Keep the household's banks, doctors, contractors and advisors in one place, linked to their records and on the phone.

## Open Contacts {#open}
@screen: CONTACTS
@done: screen
@manual: contacts#screen

In the menu, click **Contacts**, near the top.

## Gather what you already have {#gather}
@screen: CONTACTS
@target: contacts.gather
@manual: contacts#gather

Institutions, health providers, contractors, insurers and others you entered elsewhere can become contacts at once: click **Gather contacts from the app**, untick what you do not want, and click **Create the contacts**. Lines that look like the same contact can be made one.

## Add a contact {#add}
@screen: CONTACTS
@target: contacts.add
@done: shown contact.dialog
@manual: contacts#contact-form

Click **Add a contact**.

## Fill it in {#form}
@target: contact.dialog.save
@done: added contact
@manual: contacts#contact-form

- **Name**, and **Organization** or **Person**.
- **What for**: a few words that tell similar contacts apart, such as Sam's dermatologist.
- **Kinds**: such as Bank, Dentist or Contractor.
- **For whom (none chosen: the whole household)**: the people and pets it serves.
- **Add a phone**, **Add an email** and **Add an account or client number**.

Click **Save**.

## Link it to its records {#link}
@screen: CONTACTS
@target: contacts.linkRecord
@manual: contacts#link-record

On the contact's page, click **Link to a record…** to link it to an account, a bill, a vehicle, a pet, a policy and more, with its **Role**. The contact then shows beside that record on its own screen.

## On the phone {#phone}
@manual: contacts#sent-to-phone

A paired phone receives your contacts, without account numbers, on its Contacts tab. A contact added on the phone with **New contact** comes here and waits under **From the phone** at the top of this screen, to add or discard.
