# Trips on the phone
@about: Record trips with the phone, from start to arrival, and keep the CRA logbook on the computer.

## Before you start {#before}
@screen: VEHICLES
@manual: trips#from-phone

Trips need a paired phone (see the guide Pair a phone) and the vehicle on the **Vehicles** screen. The phone receives the vehicles and the saved places after its next transfer.

## Saved places {#places}
@screen: TRIPS
@target: trips.places
@manual: trips#places

In the menu, open **Home and family**, then **Trip log**. Click **Places** and add the places you go often, such as Home, the office or a client, with their kind. The phone names each end of a trip after the saved place it is at.

## On the phone: start {#start}
@manual: phone-app#trip-start

On the phone, tap **Trip** on the Capture tab. Choose the **Vehicle** and the **Driver**, check the **Odometer (km)** against the dashboard, and tap **Start**. The phone takes one location fix, now and at arrival, never in between.

## On the phone: on the way {#on-the-way}
@manual: phone-app#trip-stop

While the trip is under way, tap **Trip** again to stop at a place on the way, take a break, or add a note or a photo. A trip to one place needs none of this.

## On the phone: arrive {#arrive}
@manual: phone-app#trip-arrive

At the end, tap **Trip**, then type the **Odometer at arrival (km)**. Check the **Purpose**, suggested from the places: **Business**, **Employment**, **Medical** or **Personal**. Tap **Arrive and send**.

## On the computer: the trip log {#log}
@screen: TRIPS
@target: trips.list
@manual: trips#cards

The trip arrives in the **Trip log**, with its places, times, odometers and distance. The cards show each person's kilometres by purpose and each vehicle's work share for the year.

## The CRA logbook {#logbook}
@screen: TRIPS
@target: trips.logbook
@manual: trips#logbook

Click **Logbook**, choose the **Vehicle** and **Tax year**, and save it as **CSV**, **Excel** or **PDF** for your tax records. Medical trips can be added to medical expenses from the trip log.
