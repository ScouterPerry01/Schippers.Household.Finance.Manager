# Registered plans
@about: Follow RRSP, TFSA and FHSA room, RESP grants and the beneficiaries of each plan.

## Before you start {#before}
@screen: MEMBERS
@manual: plans#plans-before-you-start

Each plan is an account with its type (RRSP, TFSA, FHSA, RESP...) and its owner; see the guide Investment accounts and holdings. Each person needs a date of birth on **Household members**: the TFSA room, RRIF minimums and RESP grants depend on age.

Money put into a plan is a transfer from another of your accounts: that is what counts as a contribution.

## Open Registered plans {#open}
@screen: PLANS PlansTab.ROOM
@done: screen
@manual: plans#contribution-room

In the menu, open **Investing and borrowing**, then **Registered plans**. The **Contribution room** tab shows, for each person and plan, the room, what was contributed and what is left.

## Enter the CRA's figure {#cra}
@screen: PLANS PlansTab.ROOM
@target: plans.enterRoom
@manual: plans#enter-cra-figure

The room is estimated from the rules and the books. For the exact figure, click **Enter the CRA's figure**, choose the **Plan**, the **Person** and the **Year**, and type the **RRSP deduction limit** from the notice of assessment (for a TFSA, the **Room at January 1** from CRA My Account). Click **Save**.

## Over-contributions {#over}
@screen: PLANS PlansTab.ROOM
@manual: plans#over-contribution

A card shows **Over by** in red when the contributions go past the room. A contribution made outside the books can be added with **Contribution outside the books**.

## RESP grants {#resp}
@screen: PLANS PlansTab.RESP
@target: plans.recordGrant
@manual: plans#resp

On the **RESP** tab, each child shows the grants expected on the contributions and those received. When a grant arrives in the RESP, click **Record a grant received**.

## Beneficiaries {#beneficiaries}
@screen: PLANS PlansTab.BENEFICIARIES
@target: plans.addBeneficiary
@manual: plans#beneficiaries

On the **Beneficiaries** tab, click **Add** on each plan to record who receives it: a **Beneficiary** or a **Successor holder**. An RESP needs its children here before grants can be recorded. They also appear in the emergency and estate summary.
