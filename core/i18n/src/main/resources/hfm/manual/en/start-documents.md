# Getting started with documents

This chapter shows how to bring your receipts and bills into RANN's Roost, check what was read, and file each one with its payment. Each step links to the full description in [Documents](documents).

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- Documents are kept encrypted in the household folder and read on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to read a document with AI.
- Filing works best when your accounts and recent transactions are in the app, so a receipt can be matched with its payment. See [Getting started with your money](start-money).
- PDF, JPEG, PNG and HEIC files are accepted, as well as emails saved from your email program.

## Bring in your first documents {#bring-in}

1. Open **Documents** in the Money group of the menu.
2. Drag a few receipts or bills (PDFs or photos) from your computer onto the screen, or click **Import files…** and choose them.
3. Wait while the button shows **Reading…**. The first reading of a session takes a little longer.
4. A message says how many documents were added. They wait on the **To review** tab.

See [Getting documents in](documents#adding-documents).

## Check what was read {#check}

![Documents waiting for review](images/documents.png)

1. On **To review**, click **Review** on a document. The document is on the left, what was read on the right.
2. Check **Store or biller**, **Kind**, **Date** and **Total** against the picture. Fields marked "Check this: it was hard to read" deserve a close look.
3. Correct what is wrong. The app remembers your corrections for the next documents from the same store.

See [The document window](documents#document-window).

## File each document {#file}

Under **File it with**, choose one:

1. If the payment is already in your books (imported from the bank, for example), click **Attach** beside the matching transaction.
2. If it is one of your bills, click **Record it as this bill's statement**: its amount, due date, number and any meter readings are recorded on the bill for that period, and the document is kept with it. A bill that matches none of yours offers **Create a bill from this**, which fills in the bill form from the document. See [Record the amount on a bill](documents#record-on-bill).
3. If the payment is not in the books yet (a cash purchase, for example), click **New transaction from this document**, choose the account under **Paid with** and a **Category**, and click **Save**.
4. Otherwise, click **File without attaching**. You can find it later on **All documents**.

Tick **Keep this document** on warranties and receipts for things you own, so they never show up among old documents to discard.

See [Filing a document](documents#filing).

## Read hard documents with AI {#ai}

Crumpled receipts, long statements and pay stubs are often read better by AI. AI reading is optional; it uses your own Anthropic key, which is billed a small amount per document, estimated before anything is sent.

1. Turn it on and add your key under [AI reading](ai), in the Settings group of the menu.
2. In a document's window, click **Read with AI**.
3. In the window that opens, drag over anything you want to hide, such as a full account number, then click **Send**.

A statement read by AI can then be reconciled with its account, and a pay stub recorded as your pay. See [Reading a document with AI](documents#read-with-ai).

## From your phone {#phone}

With RANN's Roost Mobile, photograph receipts as you get them.

1. Pair the phone on the Phones screen, in the Settings group of the menu. See [Getting started with the phone](start-phone).
2. Take photos on the phone, or enter a quick expense. You can add a voice note.
3. While the household is open on this computer, the captures arrive on the **To review** tab. Review and file them as above.

## Scanner and e-bills {#scanner}

1. Click **Watched folder…** and **Choose folder…**, then pick the folder where your scanner saves its files, or where you save downloaded e-bills. Click **Save**.
2. From now on, every file saved there is imported within a minute and moved into an "Imported" folder inside it.
3. For an emailed receipt, save the email as a file (.eml) from your email program into the same folder, or drop it on the screen.

See [The watched folder](documents#watched-folder).
