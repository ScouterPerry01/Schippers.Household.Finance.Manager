# Getting started with investing and borrowing

This chapter gets your investments, registered plans and loans into the app with the fewest steps. Each step links to the full description in the [Investments](investments), [Registered plans](plans) and [Loans and mortgages](loans) chapters.

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

Have at hand your latest brokerage and plan statements, your latest notice of assessment (or access to CRA My Account), and your loan or mortgage agreement.

1. Under [Household members](members), enter each person's date of birth and province. Registered plan rules depend on them.
2. Under [Accounts](accounts), create one account per brokerage account, plan, crypto wallet, precious metals holding and loan. Choose the right type (Brokerage, RRSP, TFSA, FHSA, RESP, RRIF, LIF, Mortgage, Loan...) and set the owner of each.
3. For a loan or mortgage, enter what you owe as a negative opening balance.

## Record what you hold today {#record-holdings}

You do not need your whole history. Start from what you hold today:

1. Open **Investments** and choose an account.
2. Click **Add transaction**, choose the type **Units moved in**, the security (use **New security…** to create it) and the quantity.
3. In **Book cost of the units**, enter what those units cost you in all, from your statement. This is what the capital gains will be worked out from. See [Transaction fields](investments#transaction-fields).
4. Click **Save**. Repeat for each security.
5. Click **Update prices** and enter today's price of each security. See [Update prices](investments#update-prices).
6. Click **Check a statement**, enter the cash and units from your latest statement, and click **Compare**. When everything matches, click **Mark reconciled**. See [Check a statement](investments#check-statement).

> Tip: If your brokerage lets you download an OFX, QFX or CSV file, **Import statement…** brings in the transactions and securities for you. See [Import a brokerage statement](investments#import-statement).

## Keep it up to date {#keep-up-to-date}

1. Each time you buy, sell or receive a dividend, click **Add transaction** and choose the type. Or import the brokerage file each month.
2. Record money you put into a plan as a transfer from your bank account, so it counts as a contribution.
3. Update prices when you want current values, or turn on the optional price download under [Rates and prices](rates).
4. At tax time, open **Capital gains and ACB** for the year's gains and losses. See [Capital gains and ACB](investments#capital-gains-acb).

## Registered plans: room and withdrawals {#registered-plans}

1. Open **Registered plans**, tab **Contribution room**.
2. Click **Enter the CRA's figure** and copy, for each person, the RRSP deduction limit and unused contributions from the notice of assessment, and the TFSA and FHSA room from My Account. See [Enter the CRA's figure](plans#enter-cra-figure).
3. Check **Room left** on each card before you contribute. A red warning means you are over.
4. For a RRIF or LIF, open the **RRIF and LIF** tab to see the year's minimum and what is still to withdraw. See [RRIF and LIF tab](plans#rrif-lif).
5. For an RESP, add each child as RESP beneficiary under **Beneficiaries**, then follow contributions and grants on the **RESP** tab. See [RESP tab](plans#resp).

## Crypto and precious metals {#crypto-metals}

1. A crypto wallet is an account with the type Crypto wallet and the coin as currency. Choose it under **Investments** and use **Buy**, **Sell** or the other buttons, or **Import from an exchange…** for a Kraken, Coinbase, Shakepay or Newton history. See [Crypto wallets](investments#crypto-wallets).
2. If the wallet started with coins, open **Wallet details** and enter what they cost.
3. A precious metals account lists your coins and bars: click **Add coins or bars**. See [Precious metals](investments#precious-metals).

## Loans and mortgages {#loans-mortgages}

1. Open **Loans and mortgages** and choose the loan.
2. Click **Enter terms** and fill in the principal, rate, compounding, amortization, payment frequency and first payment, and for a mortgage the term end. For a renewed mortgage, use the balance, rate and remaining amortization at the start of the current term. See [Enter or edit the terms](loans#loan-terms).
3. Check that **Payment** matches your agreement; if not, enter the lender's payment.
4. Each payment, click **Record payment**, or import the payment from your bank as a transfer to the loan. See [Record payment](loans#record-payment).
5. Try **What if…** to see what an extra payment or a lump sum would save. See [What if](loans#what-if).
