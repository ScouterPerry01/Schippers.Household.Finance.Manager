# Getting started with reports

Reports answer the questions a household asks: where did the money go, are we saving, what are we worth, how did the investments do, and what goes on the tax return. This chapter takes you through your first reports in a few minutes. The full description of every report and option is in [Reports](reports).

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

@index: first report

Reports are only as good as the books behind them. Before relying on them:

1. Enter or import the transactions of the period you want to look at (see [Accounts](accounts)).
2. Give each transaction a category. Uncategorized amounts are shown apart as "(uncategorized)", so they are easy to find and fix.
3. Record moves between your own accounts as transfers, not as income or spending. Reports leave transfers out.
4. For amounts that belong to one person (a salary, a child's activities), fill in the **For** field, so the **Person** filter can show each person's share.
5. If you have accounts in another currency, check that exchange rates exist under **Rates and prices** (see [Rates and prices](rates)). A red line in a report names any currency without a rate.

## Run your first report {#first-report}

1. Open **Reports** in the **Reports and taxes** group of the menu.
2. Click **Income and expense** in the list on the left.
3. In **Period**, choose **This year** (the default) or **Last 12 months**.
4. Read the totals at the top: **Income**, **Expense** and **Net**. A positive net means you kept money.
5. In **Compare with**, choose **Same period last year** to see last year's net beside it.
6. Click **Spending by category** to see where the money went, largest first.

See [The filter bar](reports#filter-bar) for every filter.

## Dig into a figure {#dig-in}

@index: drill down

1. In **Spending by category**, click a category with a › after its name to see its subcategories.
2. Click **All categories** above the chart to go back up.
3. Click a category without subcategories: a window lists the transactions behind the figure.
4. Click a transaction in that window to open it in its account's register, to fix a category or a split.
5. Click **Show table** under the chart to see the exact amounts.

See [Charts and drill-down](reports#charts-drill-down).

## Keep and share a report {#keep-and-share}

1. Set the report and filters the way you want them, for example **Spending by category**, **Last month**, with only the cottage's accounts chosen in **Choose accounts…**.
2. Click **Save this report…**, type a name such as "Cottage", and click **Save**. It now appears under **Saved reports** on the left; one click brings it back with the latest figures.
3. To send or print it, use the buttons under the chart: **CSV**, **Excel** or **PDF** save a file, and **Print** prints it.

See [Saved and scheduled reports](reports#saved-reports) and [The table, export and print](reports#table-export).

## Build a report of your own {#own-report}

@index: custom report

1. Click **Custom report**.
2. In **Rows**, choose what each line is, for example **Payee**.
3. In **Columns**, choose **Month** to see each month side by side, or **Totals only** for one column.
4. In **Adds up**, choose **Spending**, **Income** or **Income less spending**.
5. In **Chart**, choose **Bars**, **Lines**, **Ranked bars** or **Table only**.
6. Save it with **Save this report…**. In the same window, **Make it on a schedule** can write it as a PDF to a folder of your choice every month, quarter or year.

See [Custom report](reports#custom-report) and [Scheduled reports](reports#scheduled-reports).

## Look at the whole picture {#whole-picture}

1. **Net worth** shows what you own less what you owe, month by month.
2. **Investment portfolio** shows how the investments did, with the time-weighted return and your personal rate of return, the allocation against a target, and the holdings.
3. **Debt summary** lists every loan and card with its rate and payoff date.
4. **Year in review** sums up a year against the one before: what was kept, where the money went, the biggest changes.

## Reports for tax time {#tax-time-reports}

@index: tax reports

1. Set **Tax year** to the year being filed (last year by default).
2. **Investment income and capital gains** gives the T5 and T3 amounts and the capital gains for Schedule 3; enter each slip with **Enter a slip…** when it arrives.
3. **Medical expenses** finds the best 12-month period for the medical expense tax credit, makes one PDF of the receipts, and helps choose which spouse should claim.
4. **Foreign exchange gains and currency exposure** gives the exchange gains after the $200 exemption.
5. **Registered plans** gives contribution room, RRIF and LIF minimums and RESP grants.

Then go to the Taxes screen for the slips checklist, donations, instalments and the year-end package: see [Getting started with taxes](start-taxes).
