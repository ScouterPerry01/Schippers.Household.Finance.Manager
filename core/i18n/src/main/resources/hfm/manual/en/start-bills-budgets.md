# Getting started with bills and budgets

This chapter takes you through a first setup of bills, budgets and savings goals in about half an hour. Each step links to the full description of the screen.

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- Add your accounts first (chequing, savings, credit cards), with their current balances. Bills are paid from an account, and goals are kept in one. See [Getting started with your money](start-money).
- Have a recent bank statement at hand, or your online banking open: it lists most of your regular payments.
- Budgets work best with a few months of categorized transactions. If you just started, set up bills now and come back to budgets later.

## Add your bills {#add-bills}
@index: first bills; set up bills

1. Open **Bills** in the Money group of the menu and click **Add a bill**.
2. Leave **Type** on Bill. Enter a **Name**, such as Rent or Hydro.
3. Choose the account under **Paid from** and a **Category**, such as Housing: Rent.
4. Enter the **Amount**. For a bill that changes every time, such as hydro, enter a typical amount and set **Amount is** to Variable.
5. Set **Repeats** (Monthly for most bills) and the **First due date**: the next date it is due.
6. Keep **Remind me (days before)** on 7, 1, or change it.
7. For a streaming service or another subscription, tick **Subscription**.
8. Click **Save**. Repeat for each regular bill.

Then add your pay: click **Add a bill**, set **Type** to Income, choose the account it is deposited to, and set **Repeats** to your pay schedule (often Every two weeks), with your next pay day as the first due date.

Add a regular transfer to savings the same way, with **Type** set to Transfer and the savings account under **To account**.

The details of every field are in [Add or edit a bill](bills#bill-form).

## Pay bills as they come due {#pay-bills}

1. Open the **To pay** tab of Bills. It lists what is overdue, due today and due in the next 30 days. Reminders also appear at the top of every screen.
2. When a variable bill arrives, click **Enter amount** and type its amount.
3. After you pay it through your bank, click **Mark paid**, check the date and amount, and click **Save**. The payment is recorded in the account and will match the bank statement when you import it.
4. To pass over a due date, click **Skip**; **Unskip** under Skipped brings it back. A payment marked by mistake can be reversed with **Undo** under Paid recently.

Look at the **Cash flow forecast** tab once in a while: it warns you when a payment would take an account below zero. See [The Cash flow forecast tab](bills#forecast-tab).

## Set up budgets {#set-up-budgets}
@index: first budget

1. Open **Budgets** in the Money group of the menu.
2. If you have several months of transactions, click **Suggest from last 12 months**, untick the categories you do not want to budget, and click **Create budgets**.
3. Otherwise, click **Add a budget**, pick a category such as Groceries, click **Continue**, enter the monthly amount and click **Save**.
4. For costs that come once or twice a year, such as insurance or property tax, set **Budget is** to Yearly and enter the year's amount.
5. Click any bar to adjust its budget. Tick **Carry unspent or overspent amounts into the next month** for categories where you want leftovers to roll over.

The details are in [Budgets](budgets).

## Start a savings goal {#start-goal}
@index: first savings goal

1. Open **Savings goals** in the Money group of the menu and click **Add a goal**.
2. Under **What are you saving for?**, enter for example Emergency fund, and pick the savings account under **Kept in account**.
3. Enter the **Target amount** and, if you like, a **Target date (optional)**.
4. Keep **Set an amount aside on a schedule** ticked, and enter the **Amount each time** and the schedule, matching the transfer you make to savings.
5. Click **Save**. The goal fills up on its own on the planned dates, and tells you whether you are on track.

No money moves: the goal earmarks part of the account's balance. The details are in [Savings goals](goals).

## Check in each week {#weekly-routine}

A few minutes a week keeps everything current:

1. Import your latest bank and card statements on the Accounts screen.
2. On **Bills**, mark what you paid, and enter the amounts of the bills that arrived.
3. On **Budgets**, look at the bars that are close to or over their budget.
4. On **Savings goals**, check that no account shows the goals adding up to more than its balance.
