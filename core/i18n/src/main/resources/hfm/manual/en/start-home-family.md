# Getting started with home and family

The **Home and family** group of the menu keeps everything about the people, animals and things of the household that is not an account: health, medical claims, papers for an emergency, pets, vehicles, trips and the home. This chapter gets each one going in a few steps. Do them in any order; each part works on its own, and they help each other once filled in. Family money, which is in the **Money** group, is covered at the end.

## Before you start {#before}
@index: household members; private group

1. Add everyone in the household under **Household members** in the **Settings** group: adults, children and other dependants. Health, medical claims, papers and trips are all kept per person. See [Household members](members).
2. If other people use the app with you, decide what should stay private. Health records and papers and wishes can be kept in your own private group; the forms offer **Create my private group** when you have none. See [Users](users).
3. Pay attention to the "For" and "Vehicle" fields when you enter transactions: they are how pets and vehicles get their costs.

## Health records {#health}

1. Open **Health**. Choose the person in **Person or pet**.
2. On the **Providers** tab, choose **Add a provider** for your family doctor, dentist and pharmacy.
3. On the **Allergies** tab, add any allergy, with its severity. It comes first in the health summary.
4. On the **Medications** tab, choose **Add a medication** for each one taken regularly. Fill in **Last filled** and **Days of supply** so the app can remind you before it runs out.
5. Each time you pick up a refill, choose **Record a refill**.
6. Add vaccines with their **Next dose due**, and tests with their **Follow-up date**: both appear on the calendar.
7. Choose **Health summary…** to save a protected PDF to bring to appointments.

Details: [Health](health).

## Medical claims {#medical}

1. Open **Medical claims** and go to the **Plans** tab.
2. Choose **Add a plan** for each plan: your employer's group plan, your spouse's, a Health Spending Account, RAMQ or the Canadian Dental Care Plan. Set who it covers and whether it pays first or second for each person, and its plan year.
3. Save the plan, then choose **Add coverage** for each kind of care in your benefit booklet: percentage, deductible, maximums, and how often.
4. On the **Expenses and claims** tab, choose **Add an expense** for each bill, or **From the books** to pick health payments already in your accounts. Attach the receipt.
5. Follow what the app says: **Send to** the plan it names, then **Record the payment** when the plan answers. It then proposes the next plan, if any.
6. At tax time, open the **Medical expenses** report under **Reports**: it finds the best 12-month period, helps decide which spouse should claim, and saves the receipts as one PDF.

Details: [Medical claims](medical).

## Emergency and estate {#estate}

1. Open **Emergency and estate**, tab **Papers and wishes**.
2. For each adult, note where the will, power of attorney and mandate are, the safe deposit box, where the password manager's recovery is kept, and funeral wishes. Never type a password.
3. Choose **Add a person** for the executor or liquidator, the notary and the financial advisor. Choose **Save**.
4. Attach scans of the will and mandate under **Scans (will, mandate, contracts)**.
5. On the **Emergency summary** tab, check what was gathered from your accounts, policies and plans, then **Save as PDF…** with a password. Give the file and, separately, the password to the person who would need them.

Details: [Emergency and estate](estate).

## Pets {#pets}

1. Open **Pets** and choose **Add a pet**. Enter the name, the animal, breed, date of birth and microchip number.
2. Enter the municipal licence and pet insurance with their dates: you are reminded 30 days before.
3. Choose **Health records** on the card to add vaccines and medications, and the vet under **Providers**.
4. Choose **Add appointment** for the next vet visit.
5. When you enter pet food or vet bills in the register, choose the pet under "For": the card then shows what it costs.

Details: [Pets](pets).

## Vehicles {#vehicles}

1. Open **Vehicles** and choose **Add a vehicle**. Enter the name, make, model, year, plate and VIN, the purchase with its odometer, and the registration and insurance renewal dates.
2. On the **Maintenance** tab, choose **Add usual tasks**, then adjust them to the owner's manual.
3. Choose **Enter odometer** now and then, or enter the odometer with each fill-up and service.
4. Add the warranties on the **Warranties** tab: you are reminded 60 days before each ends.
5. Log services on the **Service log** tab and fill-ups on the **Fuel** tab. Tick **Also enter the payment in an account** to record the payment at the same time.
6. Check the **Costs** tab for the cost per kilometre.

Details: [Vehicles](vehicles).

## Trips {#trips}

1. Open **Trip log** and choose **Add a trip** for each trip by car for work or medical care.
2. Enter odometer readings for the vehicle near the start and the end of the year, on the **Vehicles** screen: they give the vehicle's work share.
3. For a medical trip of 40 km or more one way, choose **Add to medical expenses**: it becomes a medical expense on the **Medical claims** screen, once; the button then reads **Added to medical expenses**.

Details: [Trip log](trips).

## Home and assets {#home-assets}

1. Open **Home and assets** and choose **Add an asset** for the home. Enter its price paid, choose **Estimate entered** as **Value**, and tick **Count in net worth**.
2. Add the appliances, electronics and valuables, each **Part of** the home. Use **Find the purchase** to link the purchase from your accounts, and attach photos and receipts under **Photos and documents**.
3. Add the warranties with **Add a warranty**.
4. In each asset, choose **Add usual tasks** for its maintenance: furnace filter, gutters, smoke detectors.
5. On the **Insurance** tab, choose **Add a policy** for the home and auto policies and tick what each covers. Check **Not covered by any policy**.
6. Record renovations on the **Projects** tab, ticking **A capital improvement** when it applies, and the people who did the work on the **Contractors** tab.
7. Each month, look at the **Maintenance** tab.

Details: [Home and assets](assets).

## Family money {#family-money}

The **Family money** screen, in the **Money** group, keeps money between people: expenses shared on a trip or with roommates, loans within the family, and children's allowances.

1. Open **Family money**.
2. On the **Shared expenses** tab, create a group for a trip or a shared apartment, then add each expense with who paid and the shares.
3. On the **Family loans** tab, record money lent between family members, then its repayments.
4. On the **Allowances** tab, set up each child's allowance and mark it paid on allowance days.

Details: [Family money](family).

## Each month {#each-month}

1. Read the reminders at the top of the window: refills, licence and insurance renewals, claims to send, warranties ending, maintenance due.
2. Open the **Maintenance** tab of **Home and assets** for what is due this month on the vehicles and the home.
3. Open **Medical claims** with **Only those still open** ticked, and send or follow up each claim.
4. Enter an odometer reading for each vehicle.
