# Getting started with taxes

The app does not file your return, but it can have everything ready for it: the slips to expect, the donation receipts, the instalments paid, and each person's figures with the line they go on. Most of it comes from entering things the right way through the year. This chapter gives the steps; the full description is in [Taxes](taxes).

> Important: The tax figures are organizational aids, not tax advice. Check them against your slips and receipts, and ask a tax professional when in doubt.

## Set up once {#set-up}

@index: tax setup

1. Add each person under **Household members**, with **Lives in** set when their province differs from the household's, so Quebec residents get their Relevés and Quebec lines (see [Household members](members)). Mark an adult who depends on you as **Other dependant**, so their medical expenses are claimed apart.
2. Set each account's owners, so amounts without a person go to the right return (see [Accounts](accounts)).
3. Check the **Tax treatment** of your categories: medical expenses, charitable donations, political contributions, child care, tuition, self-employment, employment and moving expenses (see [Categories](categories)). The default categories already have it.

## Through the year {#through-the-year}

1. Enter pay from the pay stub: in a bank account's register, start a new transaction and click **Pay stub…**. Enter the gross pay and each deduction (income tax, CPP or QPP, EI or QPIP, union dues, pension). Choose the person in **For**. See [Pay from a pay stub](taxes#pay-stub).
2. Put donations on **Charitable donations** or **Political contributions**, with the person in **For**. When the official receipt arrives, open the **Donations** tab, click the gift, enter the receipt and attach it. See [Donations](taxes#donations).
3. Enter medical expenses on the Medical claims screen, with the receipts (see [Medical claims](medical)).
4. Record contributions to RRSPs and FHSAs as transfers into the plan accounts (see [Registered plans](plans)).
5. If you are self-employed, put business income and expenses on self-employment categories, and record the GST, HST, QST or PST of each expense with **Sales tax…** in the register. See [Sales tax included](taxes#sales-tax).
6. If you pay instalments, set them up as described below, and pay them on the **Tax instalments** category.

## Pay tax instalments {#instalments-steps}

@index: instalment setup

1. When the CRA or Revenu Québec reminder arrives, open **Taxes**, then the **Instalments** tab.
2. Choose the **Tax year** and click **Set up instalments…**.
3. Choose the **Person**, **Paid to** (Canada Revenue Agency or Revenu Québec) and **Paid from** (the bank account).
4. Enter the four amounts from the reminder and click **Save**.
5. Record each payment from that account on the **Tax instalments** category; for Revenu Québec, use a payee that names it. Each instalment then shows as **Paid**, **Partly paid**, **Due** or **Late**, and unpaid ones appear among the reminders as they fall due.

See [Instalments](taxes#instalments).

## At tax time {#at-tax-time}

@index: tax season; filing

1. Open **Taxes**, then the **Slips** tab. The tax year shown is the one being prepared.
2. As each slip arrives, click it, set **Status** to **Received** and attach the slip with **Attach a file…**. Mark slips that will not come as **Not expected**. Add any slip the list does not show with **Add a slip…**. See [Slips](taxes#slips).
3. In **Reports**, open **Investment income and capital gains**, and enter each T5 and T3 with **Enter a slip…** so its boxes replace the estimate (see [Enter a slip](reports#enter-slip)).
4. Open **Medical expenses** in Reports to find the best 12-month period, and, if you have a spouse, which of you should claim (see [Which spouse should claim](reports#who-claims)).
5. Back on **Taxes**, open the **Donations** tab and clear any "Receipt missing".
6. Open the **Year-end package** tab, choose each person, and read the figures and the notes at the bottom, which list the slips still expected.
7. Click **Folder for the accountant…** to save every person's summary as PDF and Excel, with copies of their slips and receipts. Give that folder to your accountant, or use it with your tax software. See [Year-end package](taxes#year-end-package).
