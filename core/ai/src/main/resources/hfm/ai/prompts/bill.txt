This is a bill, such as electricity, gas, water, phone, internet, insurance or property tax. amount_due is the total to pay by the due date. statement_number is the bill or statement number, not the account number. For a utility bill that shows meter readings, give them in meter_readings with their dates, as printed; leave meter_readings out otherwise. When the bill is paid in instalments on set dates (a property tax bill: interim and final instalments, versements), list each one in instalments with its due date and amount, in date order, and give the first one's date as due_date; leave instalments out otherwise.
