# Your household's finances, on your own computer

RANN's Roost keeps a Canadian household's complete finances in one place: private, encrypted, and in English and French, for every province and territory.

## Private by design

Your data is encrypted with AES-256 and stays on your computer. There is no RANN account to create, no cloud, no advertising and no analytics. Optional price downloads stay off until you turn them on.

RANN's Roost does not connect to your bank: you import the statements your bank provides. Your banking passwords never go anywhere near it.

## Everything in one place

### Everyday books

- Bank accounts, credit cards, cash and loans, in any currency, with Bank of Canada exchange rates
- Import statements (OFX, QFX, QBO, CSV) and your history from Quicken, GnuCash or Moneydance
- Reconcile each statement to the cent
- Bills, subscriptions, budgets and savings goals, with reminders

### Investments and retirement

- Adjusted cost base, capital gains, and investment income for your T3, T5, RL-3 and RL-16 slips
- RRSP, TFSA, FHSA, RESP, RRIF, LIF and pensions, with contribution room and minimum withdrawals
- Mortgages with Canadian semi-annual compounding, prepayments and renewals
- Returns, asset allocation, crypto-assets and precious metals

### Home and health

- Medical plans and claims, with coordination of benefits between spouses
- Home inventory, vehicles, warranties, insurance and maintenance schedules
- Health records, pets and a calendar for appointments and renewals

### The whole household

- Several users, each with a password, and private account groups others cannot open
- Reports with charts, exported to PDF, Excel or CSV
- Encrypted backups, checked after every backup

## RANN's Roost Mobile, the companion

[Two columns: the text on the left, the image on the right.]

Photograph a receipt or bill on the spot: the phone finds the edges, straightens the page and reads the text itself. Captures wait on the phone, encrypted, and go straight to your computer over your home Wi-Fi, encrypted end to end. Nothing passes through RANN or anyone else.

The phone also shows this month's budgets, the bills coming due and the maintenance due on your home and vehicles.
