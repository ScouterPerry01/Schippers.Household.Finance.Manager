# RANN's Roost 1.0.0

The first public release of RANN's Roost, a household finance app for Canada on Windows and Linux, and RANN's Roost Mobile, its Android companion. In English and French, for every province and territory. Your household's data stays on your own computer, encrypted.

## Bookkeeping

- Bank accounts, credit cards (with supplementary cards), cash, loans, investments and registered plans, in Canadian dollars or any other currency, with Bank of Canada exchange rates.
- Import bank and card statements (OFX, QFX, QBO, CSV) and your whole history from Quicken, GnuCash or Moneydance (QIF). Categories filled in from a payee's habits are marked for review in one pass.
- Reconcile each statement to the cent: automatic matching, one line matched to several transactions or the reverse, and a saved report.
- Categories, rules, splits, transfers, named templates, changes to many transactions at once, and export of chosen transactions to CSV, QIF or OFX.
- Bills and subscriptions with reminders, budgets with rollover, savings goals inside an account, a cash flow forecast, and alerts for a low balance, a card near its limit or unusual activity.
- Each bill is Home or Business, with a category and subcategory from built-in lists you can edit, its account number with the company (masked), and every statement received with its number, dates, amount and scan. A Business bill counts as its owner's business expense, with its sales taxes, in the year-end package.
- A utility bill can be linked to its meter: each statement's readings go to the meter. A bill captured on the phone that matches no bill offers to create one, filled in from what was read.
- Pay a bill in part: the rest stays due, with reminders, until it is paid in full. Property taxes and other bills paid in instalments on set dates, rolled over to next year as estimates until the new statement comes.
- Every list with columns has a heading row, the column of buttons headed Actions; on Bills: due date, bill, amount due, to pay, outstanding.

## Documents and AI reading

- Receipts, bills and statements read on your computer (text recognition runs locally), from the phone, from saved emails or from a watched folder, kept in an encrypted vault and matched to their transactions. The app learns each store's name, kind and category from your corrections.
- Optional AI reading with your own Anthropic account and key: receipts split by item with their sales taxes, bank, card and investment statements and trade confirmations read into the books. Off until you turn it on; you see each page, can hide parts or leave pages out, and nothing is sent until you choose to send it. A usage log shows each reading.
- Documents shown page by page, with zoom in, zoom out and fit.
- Itemize a receipt or invoice by hand (item, amount, category, sales tax) into the transaction's split lines, without AI reading. Bills read on the computer or by AI fill in the account number, statement number, dates, amount, meter readings and a tax bill's instalments when printed.

## Phone

- RANN's Roost Mobile photographs receipts and bills (edges found, pages straightened, text read on the phone), takes shared PDFs, photos and email text, quick expenses, voice notes, odometer and hour readings, and new contacts.
- Captures wait on the phone, encrypted, and go to your computer over your home Wi-Fi, or through a cloud folder or email of your own, sealed end to end. Nothing passes through RANN.
- At a glance: balances, this month's budgets with alerts at 80 % and 100 %, bills due, appointments, refills, maintenance and the season's checklist, today's and tomorrow's schedules, and the household's contacts, with reminders.
- An agenda of the coming 60 days, opened from the calendar icon on the Capture and Summary tabs, with a month view: events, work and school hours, bills, refills, maintenance, seasonal tasks, renewals and fuel orders, beside the phone's own calendars.
- Trips started and ended on the phone, with a planned destination, several stops, rest breaks, and notes (typed, dictated or voice) and photos on the way. A location fix at the start, each stop, each break and the end, never in the background, with an address from a saved place or typed.
- Opt-in, off by default: an address looked up from a position through Android's geocoder, and fuel stations and EV chargers nearby from OpenStreetMap. A station can also be added by hand.
- Fuel and charging, utility meters and tanks, hours worked, chores and volunteer hours.
- Bring the calendars your phone already shows to the computer, each one private, busy only or shared; or both ways, which also writes the household's appointments, hours and bills into a calendar you choose (a phone-only RANN's Roost calendar or one of your accounts'). Medical appointments show only as "Health appointment", bills never show their amount.
- Locked by a PIN after the time you choose (at once, or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes), with fingerprint or face unlock if you wish. English or French as the phone, or as you choose. Every household member can pair their own phone.

## Investing and borrowing

- Holdings, adjusted cost base across accounts, capital gains, and investment income for your T3, T5, RL-3 and RL-16 slips; brokerage statements in CSV, OFX or read by AI.
- Time-weighted and personal rates of return, asset allocation with rebalancing, foreign exchange gains with the $200 exemption, and currency exposure.
- RRSP, spousal RRSP, TFSA, FHSA, RESP with grants, RRIF and LIF limits, pensions, CPP/QPP and OAS.
- Mortgages and loans with Canadian semi-annual compounding, prepayments, rate changes, renewals and what-if scenarios; loans between family members.
- Optional daily prices for stocks, ETFs, crypto-assets and precious metals (off by default), crypto exchange imports and watch-only Bitcoin wallets.

## Taxes

- A Taxes screen with the slips each person should receive, donations and their receipts, instalment reminders, pay stubs, and a year-end package for your return or your accountant (PDF, Excel, CSV and a folder of receipts).
- An income tax estimate for each person, federal and provincial or territorial, with Quebec's own rules: tuition, carry-forwards, the disability amount, refundable credits, the OAS recovery tax, minimum tax and Quebec's health contributions.
- Sales taxes for every province and territory kept with each purchase; refunds linked to their purchase.
- Medical expenses: the best 12-month period, Quebec's own total (TP-1 line 381), and which spouse should claim.
- Rates and rules: every rate, limit and threshold by effective date and province, with its official source, which you can review and adjust.

## Home and family

- Medical and dental plans with coordination of benefits between spouses, claims from submission to payment with their deadlines, health records, medications and refills, and a printable health summary.
- The home, appliances, vehicles, RVs, boats, pools and yards: warranties with claim logs, insurance policies and claims, maintenance schedules by time, use or season, a printable seasonal checklist, contractors, home projects, energy upgrades with rebates, and cost of ownership.
- Vehicles: service and fuel logs, consumption, a CRA logbook, km per province, inspection reminders and their share of insurance premiums.
- Utilities (electricity, gas and water meters with unusual months flagged, propane and oil tanks with a reminder to order), pets, a trip log with each trip's stops and breaks, and medical travel.
- A seasonal checklist for each change of season, across the home, cottage, vehicles, pool and yard, ticked off on the computer or the phone and recorded in the service log.
- Family money: shared expenses and settling up, allowances and paid chores; side income with invoices, hours worked and rental properties; card rewards; volunteer hours.
- Contacts: the doctors, banks, advisors and contractors you deal with, linked to the records they serve.
- An emergency and estate summary (where the will and papers are, who to call, every account and policy), saved as a protected PDF to hand over.

## Calendar

- Appointments and events with reminders on the computer and the phone, the same repeat patterns as bills, and bills, renewals, refills and maintenance shown with them.
- Agenda, day, week, month and year views, shown or hidden per kind and person.
- Work and school schedules (weekly or rotations, night shifts, holidays and exceptions) and children's activities with drivers each way and their cost.
- Calendars from each person's phone, each kept private, shown as busy only or shared, and one-time .ics imports.

## Reports

- Reports with charts, drill-down and tables: income and spending by category or payee, net worth, portfolio, investment income, registered plans, foreign exchange, medical expenses, assets and warranties, maintenance and cost of ownership, cash flow forecast, debt summary, budgets, reconciliation and the year in review.
- A custom report builder, reports by person or for chosen accounts, and saved and scheduled reports exported to PDF, Excel or CSV.

## Security and privacy

- The household is one encrypted folder (AES-256, keys protected by your password), with encrypted scheduled backups checked after every backup and a full export in open formats.
- Several users, each with their own password and role, password rules set by an administrator, private account groups other users cannot open, an activity log and automatic locking.
- No RANN account, no RANN server, no advertising, no analytics. The app contacts the internet only for the Bank of Canada rates when you use another currency, and for what you turn on (prices, AI reading, update checks). Privacy policy: https://www.rann.ca/rann-apps/rann-roost/privacy-policy-en
- A user manual with pictures in its own window, help for every screen (F1), a getting started guide, keyboard use, dark mode and text sizes.
- A Help menu with the manual, the screen's help, Walk-Me and About. About shows the version, what's new, the privacy summary, the licence and third-party notices, and copies details for support (no personal data).
- Walk-Me: step-by-step guides for common tasks, from creating the household, importing and reconciling to bills, property taxes, the phone, trips, registered plans and the year-end tax package. A guide sits at the side of the window, outlines the control to use and opens the right screen.

## Before you install

- **Windows:** from the Microsoft Store (Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit). The Store installs and updates it. To read HEIC photos from an iPhone, install Microsoft's HEIF and HEVC Video Extensions.
- **Linux:** .deb (Debian, Ubuntu), .rpm (Fedora, openSUSE) or AppImage, 64-bit x86. Each file has a `.minisig` signature and is listed in `SHA256SUMS`; check them with `minisign -Vm <file> -p release-key.pub` (the key is in the source code). These copies can check GitHub for updates once a day if you agree when first asked. To read HEIC photos, install your distribution's libheif with its HEVC plugin (such as `libheif-plugin-libde265`).
- **Android:** RANN's Roost Mobile on Google Play, or the signed APK here (Android 10 or later). It needs RANN's Roost on your computer. The Play and GitHub copies are signed differently: to switch from one to the other, uninstall first, then pair the phone again.
- Moving from a 0.9 preview: your household opens and is upgraded in place. Make a backup first.

## Known limits

- RANN's Roost does not connect to your bank: you import the statements your bank provides.
- Reading receipt line items and statements from PDFs automatically needs AI reading; on-computer reading finds the store, date, amounts and taxes, and line items can be typed with Itemize.
- Access is set per account group, not per account: an account that needs its own access goes in its own group. Members' imports need no administrator approval.
- Windows Hello unlock comes after 1.0; a password locks the household.
- The year in review compares with one previous year. The year in review, foreign exchange, investment income, budget and reconciliation reports cannot be narrowed to chosen accounts.
- Trips, contractors, home projects, invoices, rental properties and family money are kept in the first shared account group.
- The year-end package has no tax-software file format. Quebec's $200 limit for eyeglass frames is not applied. Newfoundland and Labrador's own holidays and Yukon's Heritage Day are not in the holiday calendar.
- Watch-only wallets are for Bitcoin and update when you choose Sync now. Odometer readings are typed, not read from a photo. Emails with non-Latin characters lose them in the saved PDF.
- No macOS or iPhone version.

Tax figures, including the income tax estimate, are organizational aids, not tax advice. Health records are an organizational aid, not medical advice.
