I would like to make the below modifications to Rann Roost handling of Bills.

1. BILL CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM – I want the user to be able to Type and Categorize bills as follows:
   1. Type – Either:
      1. ‘HOME’ or
      2. ‘BUSINESS’
   2. Category – For Type = HOME the following Categories and sub-categories:
      1. ‘Essential Housing & Utilities’ with following sub-categories:
         1. Utilities: Electricity, Natural Gas / Heating Oil, Water / Sewer, Trash / Recycling
         2. Housing Payments: Rent, Mortgage, Property Taxes, HOA / Condo Fees
         3. Communication: Home Internet, Cell Phone plans
      2. ‘Transportation & Mobility’ with following sub-categories:
         1. Auto Payments: Car Loans / Lease payments
         2. Vehicle Services: Auto Insurance, Parking passes, Toll accounts, Transit passes
         3. Fuel & Fleet: Gas / Charging subscriptions, Routine Maintenance & Repairs
      3. ‘Protection & Health’ with following sub-categories:
         1. Insurance: Homeowners / Renters, Health, Dental, Vision, Life, Disability, Umbrella
         2. Medical Bills: Out-of-pocket medical, Prescription subscriptions, Lab work, Therapy
      4. ‘Lifestyle & Discretionary’ with following sub-categories:
         1. Maintenance & Repair: Home repairs, Appliance maintenance, Lawn care, Pest control
         2. Vacation & Travel: Pre-funded trip accounts, Hotel / Flight installment plans, Timeshare fees
         3. Subscriptions & Services: Streaming platforms, Gym memberships, Cloud storage, News/Magazines
         4. Education & Family: Childcare / Daycare, School tuition, Extracurricular activity fees
      5. ‘Other’ with following sub-categories:
         1. Other
   3. Category – For Type = BUSINESS the following Categories and sub-categories:
      1. Facilities & Premises with following sub-categories:
         1. Occupancy: Commercial Rent / Lease, Mortgage, Property Taxes
         2. Utilities: Commercial Power, Water / Waste, Gas, Building Security / Monitoring
         3. Facilities Maintenance: Janitorial services, HVAC maintenance, Repairs, Landscaping
      2. Technology & Infrastructure with following sub-categories:
         1. Software & SaaS: Business software licenses, CRM, ERP, Cloud hosting (AWS, Azure)
         2. Telecommunications: Business Fiber / Internet, Dedicated VoIP lines, Mobile devices
         3. IT Services: Managed Service Provider (MSP) contracts, Domain registration, Cyber insurance
      3. Operations & Supply Chain with following sub-categories:
         1. Inventory & Supplies: Raw materials, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Packaging
         2. Office & Overhead: General office supplies, Postage / Shipping, Equipment leases
         3. Merchant & Bank Services: Payment processing fees, POS equipment rentals, Account maintenance
      4. Professional & Administrative Services with following sub-categories:
         1. Professional Fees: Legal retainers, Accounting / CPA fees, Business Consulting
         2. Insurance: Commercial General Liability, Cyber Risk, Errors & Omissions, Workers’ Comp
         3. Marketing & Advertising: Digital ad spend, Agency retainers, Print collateral
      5. Employee & Travel with following sub-categories:
         1. Travel & Entertainment: Client dinners, Flights, Hotels, Car rentals, Conference registrations
         2. Staff Amenities: Catered meals, Team building expenses, Employee wellness programs
      6. Other with following sub-categories:
         1. Other
2. For each Bill I would like the application to capture and store the following:
   1. Account number
   2. Statement number
   3. Issued date
   4. Due by date
   5. If the bill is categorized as a Utility bill, then capture:
      1. Previous meter reading
      2. Date of previous meter reading
      3. Current meter reading
      4. Date of current meter reading
      5. Capacity used
